# Guofeng Anime / New Guochao Style

This style is built specifically for the "Guofeng anime new Guochao" theme. All art prompts, specifications, and generated content are strictly limited to:

- **Guofeng anime animation**: An anime style set against a backdrop of classical Chinese culture ("Guofeng" — the Chinese-national aesthetic), covering genres such as xianxia (immortal fantasy), wuxia (martial arts), and court drama.
- **New Guochao aesthetic**: Traditional Eastern elements combined with a contemporary sensibility ("Guochao" — the neo-Chinese-trend aesthetic), where classical charm coexists with fashion appeal.
- **Japanese anime rendering technique**: Modern Japanese anime coloring and lighting technique, cel-shaded flat coloring combined with digital rendering.
- **Eastern classical charm**: Poetic atmosphere, negative-space composition, and an Eastern aesthetic expression of deep, evocative mood.
- **Delicate brushwork**: Clean lines, fine detail, and refined character and scene design.
- **Cinematic Guofeng anime texture**: Cinema-level composition, layered lighting, dynamic tension, and emotional atmosphere.

## Scope of Use

- AI art generation for Guofeng anime themes (characters, scenes, props, storyboards, etc.)
- Visual work requiring the new Guochao aesthetic, Japanese-style anime rendering, and a Guofeng mood
- Guofeng animation, game art, concept design, and illustration work

## Strictly Forbidden

- Fully photorealistic photography / photorealistic 3D rendering
- Western fantasy / Gothic / Victorian style
- Cyberpunk / sci-fi / excessively modern elements
- Crude linework / blurry image quality / low-precision modeling
- Chaotic color, inconsistent style, or broken aesthetics

## Style Experience

Under this style, you will experience:

- Characters in anime proportions, with refined Guofeng-era clothing and delicate, fluid linework
- Scenes full of Eastern atmosphere, rich traditional architectural detail, and refined light-and-shadow rendering
- Carefully considered props and objects, with clear material texture and harmonious, unified color
- Storyboards and rendering both emphasizing cinematic texture, the new Guochao aesthetic, and Japanese anime technique
