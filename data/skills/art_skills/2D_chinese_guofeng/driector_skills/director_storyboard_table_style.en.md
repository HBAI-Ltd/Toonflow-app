# Storyboard Table Guofeng Anime Constraints · Guofeng Anime New Guochao · Technique Reference

---

## 1. What the storyboard table is for

The storyboard table is the director's core tool for turning a script into shot language. What follows gives only the constraints of the Guofeng anime new Guochao style at the storyboard-table level.

---

## 2. Light and atmosphere

- **Unified cel-shaded lighting** — within one scene the lighting scheme must keep cel-shaded flat-coloring consistency, with shadows deepened within the same color family (hard black shadows are forbidden), unless there is an explicit narrative turn (candlelight going out → cold moonlight radiance)
- **Emotion palette binding** — every scene binds at least 1 emotional scene (xianxia ethereal/courtly splendor/wuxia severity/everyday girlhood/poetic moonlit night/festival celebration and the like), and keeps the main + secondary colors continuous within the shot group, with colors taken from the core palette (moon white #E8EAF5 / blue-green #4A9B8A / vermilion #C93752 / indigo #2B4C7E / golden yellow #D4AF37 / ink black #1A1A2E / rouge #A94A5F / ochre #965E3E / gamboge #F5E375 / gray purple #7B6C85)
- **Light angle specification** — for front-facing shots the light source defaults to a 38.5° raking soft light (cel-shaded flat-coloring texture); for side-facing shots adjust it as the frame needs. When describing light, distinguish the angle difference between front-facing and side-facing, and keep the lighting render in Japanese anime texture throughout
- **Warm-cool tone matched to the narrative stage** — wuxia severity/sorrow and lament passages use cool tones (indigo + ink black + blue-green), with warm orange/vermilion warm light forbidden; xianxia ethereal/everyday girlhood passages use soft warm tones (moon white + rouge + gamboge); courtly splendor passages use vermilion + golden yellow highlights
- **A lighting transition is an advanced device** — a gradient from light through window gauze to warm candle shadow = the passage of time from day to night. Mark the lighting change points in the storyboard table; during the transition the cel-shaded color blocks must grade smoothly, and banding in color blocks/color bleeding is forbidden
- **Japanese-style rendering light effects** — reasonable use of volumetric light, depth-of-field softening, rim light and other modern rendering techniques is allowed, but the aesthetic keynote of Guofeng anime cel-shaded flat coloring must hold; 3D photorealistic/CG-animation light effects are forbidden

---

## 3. Environmental motion

- **Environmental motion gives the frame room to breathe** — petals drifting down, cloud and mist curling, water rippling, gauze curtains stirring, sleeves and robes flowing. Every 3-4 shots, place at least one shot with environmental motion, so the frame does not go "dead"
- **Guofeng anime environmental elements first** — environmental motion should use elements from within the Guofeng world: falling blossom, drifting catkins, light smoke, running water, wind through the bamboo grove, flickering candle fire, drifting cloud and mist, points of firefly light, snowflakes falling; modern elements are forbidden
- **Cel-shaded motion texture** — environmental motion elements (petals/smoke/water ripples and so on) must keep the cel-shaded flat-coloring texture and delicate lines; 3D particle effects or photorealistic fluid effects are forbidden

---

## 4. Guofeng anime action rhythm

- **Action is elegant and animated** — character action is slow and elegant by default, but key frames may take anime exaggeration (sharp lines in a wuxia duel, hair strands lifting as a girl glances back); standing up, turning, raising a hand should be marked "slow/elegant"
- **Costume motion is a core visual asset** — the flow of Guofeng anime clothing (wide sleeves, skirt hems, silk stoles, streamers) is a natural motion asset; the motion of sleeves/hems/streamers can be added to the shot description, and under cel-shaded rendering that flowing clothing should have clear lines and distinct layers
- **Bearing constraints** — a Guofeng character's conduct should match a classical temperament: walking steady and unhurried, gestures reserved and contained, no modernised body language (shrugging, palms-up shrugs). An anime character may exaggerate slightly in micro-expression (eyes curving like crescents, cheeks flushing), but body action still needs classical restraint
- **Consistent head-to-body ratio** — in action design the character must keep the 6-7 heads classical anime proportion; breaking the head-to-body ratio for the sake of a dynamic pose is forbidden

---

## 5. Material and texture constraints

- **Cel-shaded flat coloring as the anchor** — in every shot the rendering of characters/clothing/props/scenes must keep the cel-shaded flat-coloring texture, with delicate clear lines and smooth color blocks; 3D photorealism/CG animation/photo-level rendering is forbidden
- **Clothing material** — the texture of silk + embroidery + pearlescent fabric is expressed with cel-shaded flat coloring; the weave must be ultra clear and the clothing texture clear
- **Prop material** — cold metallic sheen, translucent jade, clear wood grain, mellow porcelain, all presented with cel-shaded flat coloring + delicate brushwork; photorealistic material rendering is forbidden
- **Scene material** — use marks on wood surfaces, weathering on stone, natural creasing in fabric: materials carry a sense of age but are presented in Guofeng anime brushwork; brand-new flawless materials with a "plastic feel" are forbidden

---

## 6. Color constraints

- **Core palette locked** — the color of every shot in the storyboard table must be taken from the core palette (C1-C10), with hue shift tolerance ±8°, saturation shift ±10%, lightness shift ±12%
- **No fluorescent colors** — highly saturated fluorescent colors/neon colors/strongly digital colors are strictly forbidden in frame
- **Line color** — ink black or dark brown; pure black thick lines are forbidden
- **Shadow color** — deepened within the same color family; hard black shadows are forbidden
- **Overall color temperature** — a natural-light keynote toward neutral 5000-5600K, contrast 45-65%, saturation 55-70% (a full new-Guochao palette)

---

## 7. Camera-movement taboos

- **No fast camera movement** — whip pan (甩镜), a sudden push in, handheld shake clash with the elegant temperament of Guofeng anime new Guochao; a wuxia duel scene may pick up the pace of camera movement moderately but must still keep the frame clear
- **No showy transitions** — wipes, spins, venetian-blind transitions and the like are incompatible with this style
- **No photorealistic shot language** — handheld shake/documentary-style follow shots/fisheye lenses clash with the anime aesthetic and destroy the clean frame of cel-shaded flat coloring
