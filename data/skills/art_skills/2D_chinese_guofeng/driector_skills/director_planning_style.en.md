# Guofeng Anime Constraints · Guofeng Anime New Guochao · Technique Reference

---

## 1. Color system and the keynote of the frame

- **Color base** — the whole film sits on the core palette: moon white (C1 #E8EAF5), blue-green (C2 #4A9B8A), vermilion (C3 #C93752), indigo (C4 #2B4C7E), golden yellow (C5 #D4AF37), ink black (C6 #1A1A2E), rouge (C7 #A94A5F), ochre (C8 #965E3E), gamboge (C9 #F5E375), gray purple (C10 #7B6C85); overall color temperature toward neutral (5000-5600K), saturation medium-high (55-70%), giving frames of Eastern classical charm with a full new-Guochao palette
- **Warm-cool narrative contrast** — warm colors (vermilion C3, golden yellow C5, rouge C7, gamboge C9) are the visual signal for positive emotion/opulence/celebration; cool colors (indigo C4, ink black C6, blue-green C2) are the visual signal for conflict/severity/solitude. Warm-cool switches should track the story arc, not be mixed at random
- **Warm-cool allocation strategy** — wuxia severity/grief and isolation passages use cool tones (indigo + ink black + blue-green), with vermilion/golden-yellow warm light forbidden; xianxia ethereal/everyday girlhood passages use soft warm tones (moon white + rouge + gamboge); courtly splendor/festival celebration passages use vermilion + golden yellow highlights
- **Palette-first principle** — passage planning must bind the emotional scene first (xianxia ethereal/courtly splendor/wuxia severity/everyday girlhood/poetic moonlit night/festival celebration, etc.), then settle the main + secondary colors and the lighting scheme, to avoid "the plot is right but the emotion is the wrong color"
- **Forbidden color range** — highly saturated fluorescent colors, neon colors and strongly digital colors are all incompatible with this style. Hue shift tolerance ±8°, saturation shift ±10%, lightness shift ±12%

## 2. The lighting scheme system

- **Light is narrative** — 7 lighting schemes correspond to different emotional passages; in the directing-plan stage, settle the lighting keynote direction at passage level rather than specifying it shot by shot
- **Cel-shaded light as the anchor** — every lighting scheme must keep the cel-shaded flat-coloring texture: shadows are deepened within the same color family, hard black shadows are forbidden, and Japanese-style rendering techniques such as volumetric light/depth-of-field softening are allowed
- **Light angle** — front-facing shots default to a 38.5° raking soft light (cel-shaded flat-coloring texture); side-facing shots may adjust it as needed

| Lighting scheme | Scheme name | Tonal leaning | Suitable emotion |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Soft clear light | Moon white base + slightly warm rouge skin light | Everyday girlhood, warm sweetness, healing freshness |
| B | Ethereal immortal air | Moon white + blue-green + golden yellow rim light | Xianxia ethereal, otherworldly mood, a stunning entrance |
| C | Warm candle shadow | Ochre warmth dominant + ink black darks | Night ambiguity, intimacy warming, lingering warmth |
| D | Cold moonlight radiance | Indigo + moon white cool light | Poetic moonlit night, longing in solitude, serene and cold |
| E | Light through window gauze | Moon white base + dappled side light | Interior of a lady's chamber, everyday study, daytime stillness |
| F | Courtly splendor | Vermilion + golden yellow highlights + ink black darks | Courtly splendor, grand celebration, formal solemnity |
| G | Cool-toned severity | Indigo + ink black + 38.5° raking cool light, no warm color | Wuxia duel, grief and isolation, tense atmosphere |

- **Warm-cool light allocation** — warm light (ochre warmth/vermilion/golden yellow) suits positive-emotion/opulent passages; wuxia severity/grief passages take cool light throughout. The director may adjust the warm-cool switch points as the narrative needs
- **Atmosphere direction mapping** — the atmosphere direction of every scene should map onto one direction among the lighting schemes above (A-G), to guarantee visual consistency

## 3. Texture direction

- **Cel-shaded flat coloring as the anchor** — the core of Guofeng anime: delicate lines, cel-shaded flat coloring, Japanese anime rendering, smooth gradation within color blocks; banding in color blocks/color bleeding is forbidden
- **Delicate brushwork + cinematic texture** — this is the anchor of the frame's texture. Not photorealistic photography, not 3D CG, but a fusion of anime Japanese-style rendering with Guofeng aesthetics
- **Materials rendered in anime terms** — the material of every costume, prop and set must be presented with cel-shaded flat coloring: the sheen of silk through smooth color blocks, the reflection of metal through cold-sheen lines, the mellowness of jade through inner-glow color blocks. A "3D feel" and a "photorealistic feel" are forbidden
- **Skin texture** — pink-white key, cel-shaded flat coloring, natural sheen, soft texture, inner glow, translucent glowing skin. Matte/dead white/waxy/greasy shine/overexposed are forbidden
- **Anime does not mean flat** — Guofeng anime new Guochao emphasises building spatial and cinematic feeling on top of cel-shaded flat coloring through layered light, depth-of-field softening and volumetric light, rather than a flat cartoon style with no depth

## 4. Guofeng anime scene spatial elements

Scene elements particular to the Guofeng world and their visual narrative function:

- **Gauze curtain/screen/door frame/carved window lattice** — natural framing props for composition, creating a "can't see through it" sense of layering and spatial depth, with refined carving detail rendered in cel-shaded lines
- **Courtyard/flowering trees/curtain of rain/blue-green landscape** — natural vehicles for negative-space composition, where the scene is the feeling: a courtyard full of blossom = release, sitting alone in the rain = solitude, leaves falling = the sorrow of parting, drifting cloud and mist = the ethereal air of the immortals
- **Candle fire/moonlight/window light/lantern** — the light-carrying vehicles of the Guofeng world: candlelight = warm/private (scheme C), moonlight = cool/solitary (scheme D), window light = everyday/still (scheme E), lantern = celebration/marketplace (scheme F)
- **Flying eaves and dougong brackets/vermilion palace walls/stone arch bridge/classical furniture** — architectural elements presented with the new Guochao aesthetic, rich in detail, with clear cel-shaded lines and materials carrying natural wear/the patina of years; a brand-new flawless "plastic feel" is forbidden
- **Bridge passages with scene-only shots** — this style has a rich stock of scene assets (variants across times of day/weather/shot sizes), so use scene-only shots as an emotional buffer between passages rather than cutting hard
- **Turning points through visuals, not dialogue** — prefer visual means (an abrupt light change, a shot-size jump cut, an empty-shot metaphor) over relying on dialogue to explain
- **Foreground, middle-ground and background layers** — every scene must have a foreground/middle ground/background; flatness is ruled out, and exteriors must include atmospheric perspective (distant hills dark as painted brows)

## 5. Ancient-style instruments and ambient sound

Constraints on the sound elements of the ancient-style world:

### Instrument choice

- **Xiao (vertical bamboo flute)** — the core instrument for desolate, solitary and grieving passages; fits the poetic moonlit night/xianxia ethereal atmosphere closely
- **Erhu** — for passages of surging emotion, grief and longing; the crying quality of the bowed strings suits an emotional outburst
- **Suona** — for passages of violent emotional swing (great grief or great joy, a turn of fate, a climax); use sparingly, but it lands like a bomb
- **Guqin** — setting the key at the opening / steady passages / a xianxia mood; used together with the xiao
- **Pipa** — an accent for tense, urgent passages, the rhythm driver of a wuxia duel scene; never the lead
- Strings underneath can add cinematic feeling but must not upstage the rest

### Instrument combination strategy

| Emotional stage | Instrument combination |
|---|---|
| Steady/opening/closing | Guqin solo, or guqin + xiao |
| Xianxia/otherworldly/mood | Xiao + guqin + light bells |
| Grief thickening | Xiao + erhu |
| Emotional outburst/turn of fate | Suona solo, or suona + erhu |
| Tense/urgent/wuxia | Pipa accents + drum beats + strings underneath |
| Everyday girlhood/sweetness | Guzheng + bamboo flute |
| Court/celebration/grandeur | Bianzhong chime bells + guzheng + strings underneath |

### Ancient-style ambient sound

- **Typical ambient sound layers** — cicadas and insects singing / a stream babbling / wind through the bamboo grove / market hawkers calling / night rain dripping from the eaves / the rustle of fabric / a wind chime ringing lightly / petals drifting down / the faint sound of fireflies' points of light
- **Mark 1-2 core ambient sounds for every scene**, to help the later sound effect design. The richer the ambient sound layers, the more immersive the Guofeng scene
