# Stop-Motion Clay Animation Style

This style is built specifically for the "stop-motion clay texture" theme. All art prompts, specifications, and generated content are strictly limited to:

- **Stop-motion animation world**: Set against a retro/whimsical backdrop, covering log cabins, forests, streets, cafes, gardens, etc., conveying the distinctive life atmosphere of stop-motion animation.
- **Clay texture**: All characters, scenes, and props follow stop-motion clay animation as the standard, emphasizing clay-surface clarity, visible fingerprint indentations, and material granularity.
- **Healing, nostalgic aesthetic**: Building on the clay texture, the style pursues a cinematic mood — soft, warm color tones, rich detail, and an overall warm, healing, nostalgic and moving atmosphere.
- **Emotional expression**: Suited to gentle, healing, romantic stop-motion stories, emphasizing emotional expression through a handcrafted texture.

## Scope of Use

- AI art generation for stop-motion clay themes (characters, scenes, props, storyboards, etc.)
- Visual work requiring an authentic clay texture, delicate light-and-shadow, and a warm, healing atmosphere

## Strictly Forbidden

- Modern 3D animation style, smooth plastic, CG rendering
- Modern elements, modern architecture, modern clothing
- Highly saturated fluorescent colors, neon colors
- Dark/violent content, adult content

## Style Experience

Under this style, you will experience:

- Characters with delicate faces and a gentle or clear temperament; clothing and styling both in the stop-motion clay animation style
- Scenes with rich spatial layering, clear clay texture, and an atmosphere that is authentic and poetic
- Props, everyday objects, and environmental details all in retro/whimsical form, with a delicate handcrafted texture
- Storyboards and frames both built on stop-motion animation, emphasizing narrative and emotional expression

---

This style applies only to "stop-motion clay" related projects. For other styles (such as modern 3D, CG, illustration, or contemporary styles), please do not use this prompt set.
