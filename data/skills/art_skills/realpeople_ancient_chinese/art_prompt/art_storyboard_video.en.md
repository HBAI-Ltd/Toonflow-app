# Video Prompt · Visual Style Constraints

When generating a video prompt, you must inject the following visual style tags:

| Mode | Style tag |
|------|----------|
| **Universal multi-reference mode (English)** | `Chinese period drama, photorealistic, cinematic, high contrast, ultra-fine detail` |
| **Universal first/last-frame mode (English)** | `Chinese period drama, photorealistic, cinematic, high contrast, ultra-fine detail, shallow depth of field` |
| **Seedance 2.0 (English)** | `ancient-style realistic photography, cinematic style, high contrast, extreme detail` |
