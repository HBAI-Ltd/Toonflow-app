# Ancient-Style Constraints · Live-Action Ancient Chinese Realism · Technique Reference

---

## 1. Tonal system and visual keynote

- **Tonal base** — the whole film takes moon white (C1), cool fair skin (C2) and indigo blue-gray (C6) as its base colors, with an overall cool color temperature (4800-5200K) and medium-low saturation (30-50%), giving a desolate, cool, refined gray tonality
- **Warm-cool narrative contrast** — warm colors (amber warm C7, pearl gold C3, rosy-mist pink C5) act as the visual signal of a narrative turn, used as local accents in passages where the feelings warm up. Warm-cool switches must be synchronized with the story arc, not mixed at random
- **Warm-cool allocation strategy** — grief/isolation/oppression passages strictly maintain the cool-toned base, with warm tones such as warm orange and amber forbidden; warm colors are released only after the feelings warm up, and the warm-cool contrast carries the narrative turn
- **Palette-first principle** — passage planning must bind the emotional scene first (first sight/rising tension/misunderstanding/reunion/grand wedding and the like), then settle the main + secondary colors and the lighting scheme, to avoid "the plot is right but the emotion is in the wrong color"
- **Forbidden color range** — highly saturated fluorescent colors, neon colors and modern color systems are all incompatible with this style

---

## 2. Lighting-scheme system

- **Light is narrative** — 7 lighting schemes correspond to different emotional passages; at the director-planning stage, settle the direction of the lighting keynote at passage level rather than specifying it shot by shot
- **Light-source angle** — a front-facing shot is 38.5° raking by default (not 45°); a side-facing shot may be adjusted as appropriate

| Lighting scheme | Scheme name | Tonal leaning | Suitable emotion |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Pearl soft diffusion | Cool white base + slightly warm skin light | Everyday warmth, light-hearted sweetness |
| B | Side-backlit ethereal air | Moon white + pearl gold rim light | Fairyland reveal, stunning entrance |
| C | Candlelight warm shadow | Amber warm dominant + ink-jade black dark areas | Nighttime tension, intimacy rising |
| D | Cold moonlight radiance | Indigo blue-gray + frost-snow silver | Nighttime solitude, longing in isolation |
| E | Light through window gauze | Cool white base + dappled side light | Interior daytime, everyday living |
| F | Diffused skylight | Indigo blue-gray distant scenery + moon white mist | Long shot (远景), in the mist, atmospheric empty shots |
| G | Cool raking light | Indigo blue-gray and cool white + 38.5° raking cool light, no warm color | Grief and isolation, oppressive desolation |

- **Warm-cool light allocation** — warm light (amber warm/warm orange) suits passages where the feelings warm up; grief/isolation passages use cool light without exception. The director may adjust the warm-cool switch points as the narrative requires
- **Atmosphere-direction mapping** — the atmosphere direction of every scene should map onto one of the directions of the lighting schemes above (A-G), to guarantee visual consistency

## 3. Texture direction

- **Ultra-crisp documentary feel** — the core of live-action realistic photography: visible pores, hair strands separated one by one, ultra-crisp texture detail
- **High contrast + extreme detail** — this is the anchor of the visual texture. Not film grain, not ink-wash impressionism, but film/TV-grade documentary photography
- **Authentic materials** — the materials of all costumes, makeup and props must be believable: the sheen of silk, the weave of cotton and linen, the reflection of metal, the moist feel of jade. "Plastic feel" and "CG feel" are forbidden
- **Skin texture** — the pores, small blemishes and sheen of real skin. Neither over-smoothed nor deliberately exaggerating flaws; aim for "real but good-looking"
- **Realism is not blandness** — live-action ancient Chinese realism emphasizes an image language that could really be shot (real light positions, real materials, real action rhythm), amplifying impact through shot and emotion design rather than relying on spectacle effects

## 4. Ancient-style scene-space elements

Scene elements unique to the ancient Chinese world and their visual-narrative function:

- **Gauze curtain/folding screen/door frame** — natural framing props that create a "can't see through" sense of layering and spatial depth
- **Courtyard/flowering tree/curtain of rain** — natural carriers of negative-space composition; the scene is the feeling: a courtyard full of blossom = release, sitting alone in the rain = solitude, falling leaves scattering = the sorrow of parting
- **Candle flame/moonlight/window light** — the light-source carriers of the ancient Chinese world; candlelight = warm/intimate (scheme C), moonlight = cool/lonely (scheme D), window light = everyday/quiet (scheme E)
- **Use empty scene shots to transition between passages** — this style has rich scene assets (variants by time of day/weather); for passage joins, an empty scene shot is recommended as an emotional buffer rather than a hard cut
- **Turning points use visuals, not dialogue** — prefer visual means (an abrupt lighting change, a jump cut in shot size, an empty-shot metaphor) over relying on dialogue to explain

## 5. Ancient-style instruments and ambient sound

Constraints on sound elements within the ancient Chinese world:

### Instrument choice

- **Xiao (vertical bamboo flute)** — the core instrument for bleak, lonely and grief-stricken passages; nothing expresses cold desolation and plaintive sorrow better
- **Erhu** — for passages of surging feeling, grief and longing; the sobbing quality of the bowed strings suits an emotional outburst
- **Suona** — for passages of violent emotional swing (great sorrow and great joy, a turn of fate, a climax); use it sparingly, but once used it hits like a bomb
- **Guqin** — for setting the key at the opening / for steady passages, used together with the xiao
- **Pipa** — an accent for tense, urgent passages; never the lead
- A string bed can add a cinematic feel but must not steal the show

### Instrument combination strategy

| Emotional stage | Instrument combination |
|---|---|
| Steady/opening/closing | Guqin solo, or guqin + xiao |
| Grief deepening | Xiao + erhu |
| Emotional outburst/turn of fate | Suona solo, or suona + erhu |
| Tense/urgent | Pipa accents + string bed |

### Ancient-style ambient sound

- **Typical ambient-sound layers** — cicadas and insects / a babbling stream / wind through a bamboo grove / street vendors calling / night rain dripping from the eaves / fabric rustling / a wind chime ringing lightly
- **Mark 1-2 core ambient sounds for every scene**, to help the later sound design. The richer the ambient-sound layering, the more immersive the ancient-style scene
