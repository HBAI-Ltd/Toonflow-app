# Live-Action Ancient Chinese Realism Style

This style is built specifically for the "live-action ancient Chinese realism" theme. All art prompts, specifications, and generated content are strictly limited to:

- **Ancient Chinese world**: Set against a backdrop of ancient China, covering residences, palaces, objects, clothing, weapons, etc., with modern elements strictly excluded.
- **Live-action realism**: All characters, scenes, and props follow live-action realistic photography as the standard, emphasizing extreme detail and authentic texture in facial features, skin, hair strands, and material.
- **Ancient realist documentary feel**: Building on realism, the style pursues a film/TV-level atmosphere — strong light-and-dark contrast, rich detail, and an overall mood that is cool with warmth, sparse yet dense.
- **Sweet, tender atmosphere**: Suited to gentle, healing, romantic ancient-style love stories, emphasizing emotional expression through an Eastern aesthetic.

## Scope of Use

- AI art generation for live-action ancient-style themes (characters, scenes, props, storyboards, etc.)
- Visual work requiring extreme realism, delicate texture, and a classical Eastern atmosphere

## Strictly Forbidden

- Cartoon, anime, illustration, or 2D anime style
- Modern elements, modern clothing, modern scenes
- Highly saturated fluorescent colors, neon colors
- Suggestive, exposed, or see-through content or other unsuitable material

## Style Experience

Under this style, you will experience:

- Characters with delicate faces and a cool or gentle temperament; clothing, makeup, and hairstyles all in ancient-style realism
- Scenes with rich spatial layering, ultra-crisp material texture, and an atmosphere that is authentic and poetic
- Props, weapons, and accessories all in ancient-style form, finely crafted with an exquisite texture
- Storyboards and video both built on live-action realism, emphasizing narrative and emotional expression

---

This style applies only to "live-action ancient Chinese realism" related projects. For other styles (such as illustration, anime, or contemporary styles), please do not use this prompt set.
