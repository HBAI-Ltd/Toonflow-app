# 1990s Japanese Anime Style

This style is built specifically for the "1990s Japanese anime texture" theme. All art prompts, specifications, and generated content are strictly limited to:

- **1990s anime world**: Set against a backdrop of 1990s Japanese daily life, covering schools, train stations, streets, cafes, homes, etc., conveying the atmosphere of Japanese life at the time.
- **Hand-drawn flat-color texture**: All characters, scenes, and props follow 1990s hand-drawn animation as the standard, emphasizing line clarity, even flat coloring, and layered light-and-shadow.
- **Nostalgic, healing aesthetic**: Building on flat coloring, the style pursues a cinematic mood — soft, warm color tones, rich detail, and an overall warm, healing, nostalgic and moving atmosphere.
- **Emotional expression**: Suited to gentle, healing, romantic Japanese love stories, emphasizing emotional expression through an Eastern aesthetic.

## Scope of Use

- AI art generation for 1990s Japanese anime themes (characters, scenes, props, storyboards, etc.)
- Visual work requiring an authentic hand-drawn texture, delicate linework, and a warm, healing atmosphere

## Strictly Forbidden

- Modern Japanese anime style, 3D rendering, CG animation
- Modern elements, modern architecture, modern clothing
- Highly saturated fluorescent colors, neon colors
- Excessive shadow, dark/violent content, adult content

## Style Experience

Under this style, you will experience:

- Characters with delicate faces and a gentle or clear temperament; clothing, makeup, and hairstyles all in the 1990s Japanese anime style
- Scenes with rich spatial layering, clean and fluid linework, and an atmosphere that is authentic and poetic
- Props, everyday objects, and environmental details all styled in the Japanese manner, finely crafted with a delicate texture
- Storyboards and frames both built on hand-drawn flat coloring, emphasizing narrative and emotional expression

---

This style applies only to "1990s Japanese anime" related projects. For other styles (such as modern animation, CG, illustration, or contemporary styles), please do not use this prompt set.
