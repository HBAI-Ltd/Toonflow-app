# Storyboard-Table Flat Style Constraints · 2D Flat Design · Technique Reference

---

## 1. Where the storyboard table sits

The storyboard table is the director's core tool for turning the script into camera language. What follows provides only the constraint specification of the 2D Flat Design style at storyboard-table level.

---

## 2. Color and atmosphere

- **One color scheme per scene** — no more than two color schemes should appear within a single scene, unless there is an explicit narrative turn (for example warm color blocks suddenly switching to cool ones)
- **Emotion-palette binding** — every scene binds at least 1 emotional scene (such as everyday warmth/heart-flutter moment/romantic scene/quiet night) and keeps the main + secondary colors continuous within the shot group
- **Color-contrast specification** — medium contrast by default; when describing it, distinguish high contrast from low contrast
- **Match warm/cool tone to the narrative stage** — everyday passages keep a warm tonal base; cool tones are used at night or after an emotional turn
- **Color transition is an advanced device** — gradating from warm to cool = a shift of feeling. Mark the color change points in the storyboard table

---

## 3. Color-block dynamics

- **Color-block change gives the frame room to breathe** — hue change, lightness change, size change. Every 3-4 shots, schedule at least one shot with a color-block change so the frame does not go "dead"
- **Flat-style environmental elements come first** — color-block dynamics should use elements from within the flat style: hue gradation, color-block displacement, lightness transition, geometric-shape change; realistic/3D elements are forbidden

---

## 4. Flat action rhythm

- **Action must stay simple** — all character action defaults to a natural rhythm. Raising a hand or turning around should both be marked "simple" or "smooth"
- **No more than two actions in a single shot** — "raise a hand + smile" is fine; "raise a hand + smile + turn around + walk" will collapse
- **Sweet-romance interaction is implied** — meeting gazes, a gesture, handing over an object. Do not write large two-person interactions such as "hug" or "kiss"

---

## 5. Camera-movement taboos

- **Complex camera movement is banned** — rotating, warping and fast switching clash with the temperament of this style
- **Fancy transitions are banned** — rotation, warp, wipe and the like are incompatible with this style
- **Static (静止) by default** — over 70% of shots should be a locked-off camera (固定机位), letting the frame's color blocks and composition speak for themselves
