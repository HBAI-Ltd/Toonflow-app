# 2D Flat Design Style

This style is built specifically for the "2D Flat Design" theme. All art prompts, specifications, and generated content are strictly limited to:

- **Flat design style**: Centered on Flat Design, covering UI design, infographics, illustration, etc., embodying a modern, minimalist aesthetic.
- **Solid color-block texture**: All characters, scenes, and props follow the 2D flat style as the standard, emphasizing solid-color fills, no shadow and no gradient, and clean lines.
- **Minimalist, modern aesthetic**: Building on the flat style, the goal is clear expression — bright, clean color tones, simplified detail, and an overall minimalist, modern, comfortable atmosphere.
- **Emotional expression**: Suited to gentle, healing, romantic flat-style content, emphasizing emotional expression through a modern minimalist aesthetic.

## Scope of Use

- AI art generation for 2D flat-style themes (characters, scenes, props, storyboards, etc.)
- Visual work requiring an authentic flat texture, clean lines, and a modern minimalist atmosphere

## Strictly Forbidden

- Photorealistic rendering, 3D rendering, photo-level realism
- Shadow, gradient, texture, light-and-shadow
- Complex detail, fine texture, realistic backgrounds
- Dark/violent content, adult content

## Style Experience

Under this style, you will experience:

- Characters with a minimalist, fresh-looking face and temperament; styling and clothing in the 2D flat design style
- Scenes with clean spatial layering, clear and fluid linework, and an atmosphere that is authentic and modern
- Props, everyday objects, and environmental details all in flattened form, simply crafted with a clean texture
- Storyboards and frames both built on 2D flat style, emphasizing narrative and emotional expression

---

This style applies only to "2D Flat Design" related projects. For other styles (such as realistic 3D, illustration, or modern 3D), please do not use this prompt set.
