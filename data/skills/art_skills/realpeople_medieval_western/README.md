# Medieval Epic — Live-Action

This style is built exclusively for the theme of **live-action medieval Western epic drama**. All art prompts, standards, and generated content are strictly bounded by:

- **A medieval Western European worldview**: castles, great halls, hunters' guild lodges, taverns, forges, moorlands, old forests, mountain passes, village markets. A secular world of lords, guilds, and common folk — absolutely no modern elements, and no religious institutions of any kind.
- **Live-action photorealism**: every character, scene, and prop is anchored to real cinema photography — pore-level skin detail, weathered leather and wool, forged iron, mud and frost. HBO-grade period drama image quality; absolutely no CG-render, anime, or illustration feel.
- **A grim, restrained epic aesthetic**: desaturated grading (30–50%), a cold base with warmth only from fire; strong tonal contrast, deep shadow detail, cinematic atmosphere. The overall feeling is somber, weighty, and quietly humane — an ember of warmth inside a cold world.
- **Emotional register**: built for stories of a broken hero, a single bond worth dying for, betrayal, and public vindication — including bonds with nonhuman characters (elves, beasts) rendered with practical-effects realism.

## Scope of application

- AI image generation for live-action medieval epic subjects (characters, scenes, props, storyboards...)
- Works that require maximum photorealism, weathered material texture, and a grim epic atmosphere
- Stories centered on hunters' guilds, lords' courts, exile, monster-hunting, and cross-species bonds

## Strictly forbidden content

- Anime, 2D illustration, 3D render, CG or game-engine texture of any kind
- Modern elements: modern clothing, architecture, technology, grooming
- East Asian architecture, costume, or guofeng aesthetics
- Churches, temples, clergy, crosses, or any religious symbols and institutions
- High-fantasy gloss: neon or fluorescent color, glowing rainbow magic, polished gold armor
- Graphic gore: fresh blood, open wounds, dismemberment — violence is conveyed by implication, aftermath, and grading, never by explicit injury (this also protects generation from content-filter rejection)

## What this style delivers

- Faces with pore-level realism, weathered by wind and hardship; healed scars, dirt, and fatigue that read as lived history — never fresh wounds
- Costume tiers from serf's wool to noble's fur-trimmed cloak, every fabric showing wear: mud-caked hems, frayed cuffs, rust at armor joints
- Scenes with deep spatial layers, motivated light (window, fire, torch, overcast sky), and weather as a constant presence — fog, rain, snow, breath visible in cold air
- Nonhuman characters (elves, beasts) with plausible anatomy and practical-effects realism, never glossy CG creatures
- Storyboards and video built on the same photographic base, emphasizing storytelling, restraint, and emotional weight
