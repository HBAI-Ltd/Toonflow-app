# Live-Action Urban Realism Style

This style is built specifically for the "live-action urban realism" theme. All art prompts, specifications, and generated content are strictly limited to:

- **Modern world**: Set against a modern urban backdrop, covering offices, cafes, homes, streets, malls, etc., with missing modern elements strictly excluded.
- **Live-action realism**: All characters, scenes, and props follow live-action realistic photography as the standard, emphasizing extreme detail and authentic texture in facial features, skin, hair strands, and material.
- **Urban realist documentary feel**: Building on realism, the style pursues a film/TV-level atmosphere — strong light-and-dark contrast, rich detail, and an overall mood that is direct, warm, and full of modern life.
- **Sweet, tender atmosphere**: Suited to gentle, healing, romantic urban love stories, emphasizing emotional expression through a modern urban setting.

## Scope of Use

- AI art generation for live-action urban themes (characters, scenes, props, storyboards, etc.)
- Visual work requiring extreme realism, delicate texture, and a modern urban atmosphere

## Strictly Forbidden

- Cartoon, anime, illustration, or 2D anime style
- Missing modern elements, ancient style / period costume / traditional architecture
- Highly saturated fluorescent colors, neon colors
- Suggestive, exposed, or see-through content or other unsuitable material

## Style Experience

Under this style, you will experience:

- Characters with delicate faces and a gentle or capable temperament; clothing, makeup, and hairstyles all in modern urban realism
- Scenes with rich spatial layering, ultra-crisp material texture, and an atmosphere that is authentic and full of everyday life
- Props such as phones, computers, and office supplies all in modern form, finely crafted with an exquisite texture
- Storyboards and video both built on live-action realism, emphasizing narrative and emotional expression

---

This style applies only to "live-action urban realism" related projects. For other styles (such as ancient style, illustration, anime, or contemporary styles), please do not use this prompt set.
