# Urban Style Constraints · Live-Action Urban Realism · Technique Reference

---

## 1. Tone system and visual keynote

- **Tonal base** — the whole piece uses off-white (C1), warm skin tone (C2) and refined gray (C6) as its base colors, with an overall warm-leaning color temperature (5200-5800K) and medium-low saturation (40-60%), giving a warm, bright sense of urban life
- **Warm-cool narrative contrast** — warm colors (warm amber C7, cream pink C5, metallic silver C8) act as the visual signal of emotional warming and relationship confirmation; cool colors serve passages of pressure, misunderstanding and estrangement. Warm-cool switching must stay in sync with the emotional arc
- **Warm-cool distribution strategy** — everyday and work passages stay mainly neutral/slightly cool; intimacy and reconciliation passages release warm colors; avoid keeping everything warm throughout, which collapses the emotional layering
- **Palette-first principle** — passage planning binds the emotional scene first (meeting/ambiguity/date/argument/reconciliation, etc.), then settles the main color + secondary color and the lighting scheme, avoiding "the plot is right but the emotion is the wrong color"
- **Banned color range** — highly saturated fluorescent colors, heavy neon pollution and excessive cyber coloring are incompatible with this style


---

## 2. Lighting scheme system

- **Light is narrative** — the 6 lighting schemes correspond to different emotional passages; at the director planning stage decide the lighting keynote direction at passage level, not shot by shot
- **Light angle** — for shots facing the character straight on, light falls at 30° off-axis by default (softer); for shots from the side, fine-tune it according to the scene and the character relationship

| Lighting scheme | Scheme name | Tonal tendency | Applicable emotion |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Natural diffuse | Off-white base + even soft light | Everyday commuting, slice-of-life narrative |
| B | Warm side light | Cream warm + local warm light | Warming interaction, relaxed at home |
| C | Top light and ambient mix | Neutral gray + soft cool white | Workplace rhythm, rational communication |
| D | Window light with point sources | Warm amber dominant + cool blue background | Night ambiguity, intimacy building |
| E | Street neon glow | Cool background + warm accents | Urban night view, romantic wandering |
| F | Overcast diffuse cool light | Gray-blue keynote + local warm | Transition passages, low points |

- **Warm-cool light distribution** — warm light may be used throughout but must be controlled layer by layer; lower the share of warm light in conflict/estrangement passages, so emotion and light color do not clash
- **Atmosphere direction mapping** — the atmosphere direction of every scene should map onto one of the lighting schemes above (A-F), to keep visual consistency

## 3. Texture direction

- **Ultra-crisp documentary feel** — the core of live-action realistic photography: skin texture visible, every hair strand distinct, material detail clear and believable
- **Strong contrast + extreme detail** — the anchor of visual texture. Not low-contrast pastel freshness, not heavy-filter mood-making, but a film/TV documentary feel that can actually be shot and delivered
- **True materials** — costume, makeup and props need real-world credibility: suit fabric, glass reflection, metal fittings, wooden furniture and the highlights on electronic screens should all follow real urban shooting logic
- **Skin texture** — keep real pores and fine imperfections, control the strength of skin smoothing, aim for "real but good-looking"
- **Realistic does not mean bland** — amplify emotional impact through composition, shot size and movement rhythm rather than leaning on exaggerated effects and heavy post-production

## 4. Urban scene spatial elements

Scene elements specific to the urban world and their visual narrative functions:

- **Window frames/glass/doorways** — natural framing props for composition, creating spatial layering and a sense of distance between characters
- **Streets/metro/lifts/office corridors** — carriers of urban rhythm; switching between commuting and pausing maps directly onto a character's psychological state
- **Cafes/offices/home living rooms** — emotional containers: cafes lean toward probing, offices toward restraint, home toward safety and relaxation
- **Weather and time of day as transitions** — day to night, clear to rain, dusk into night can buffer the emotion between passages, avoiding hard cuts
- **Carry turning points visually, not by explanation** — prefer visual means (an abrupt change of shot size, misaligned eyelines, spatial blocking) to carry an emotional turn, reducing the burden of explanation on the dialogue

## 5. Urban instruments and ambient sound

Constraints on the sound elements of the urban world:

### Instrument choice

- **Piano** — the core instrument for everyday, warm and memory passages; best at conveying the texture of urban life
- **Guitar** — an effective lead for relaxed, street-level, slow-tempo romantic passages
- **Strings** — the main bed for passages of rising emotion and emotional release
- **Lo-fi/light electronic** — a style booster for youthful passages, city nightlife and rhythm switches
- **Ambient sound has high priority** — in an urban drama, real ambient sound is the key to immersion; the score must not overpower the dialogue

### Instrument combination strategy

| Emotional stage | Instrument combination |
|---|---|
| Calm/opening/closing | Solo piano or solo guitar |
| Everyday development | Piano + light percussion |
| Ambiguity warming up/romance | Piano + strings |
| Emotional release/conflict | Strings leading + low-frequency bed |
| Night view/transition | Lo-fi/light electronic + ambient sound |

### Urban ambient sound

- **Typical ambient sound layers** — traffic / metro arrival announcement / lift chime / coffee machine / keyboard typing / rain / air-conditioning floor noise / distant city noise
- **Mark 1-2 core ambient sounds per scene**, to help later sound design. The more accurate the ambient sound layers, the stronger the urban realist texture
