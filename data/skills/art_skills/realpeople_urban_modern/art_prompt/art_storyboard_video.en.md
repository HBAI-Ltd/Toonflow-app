# Video Prompt · Visual Style Constraints

When generating a video prompt, you must inject the following visual style tags:

| Mode | Style tag |
|------|----------|
| **Universal multi-reference mode (English)** | `modern urban drama, photorealistic, cinematic, natural lighting, ultra-fine detail` |
| **Universal first/last-frame mode (English)** | `modern urban drama, photorealistic, cinematic, natural lighting, ultra-fine detail, shallow depth of field` |
| **Seedance 2.0 (English)** | `urban realistic photography, cinematic style, natural lighting, extreme detail` |
