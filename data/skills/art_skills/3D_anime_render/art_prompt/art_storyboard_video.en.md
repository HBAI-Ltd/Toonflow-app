# Video Prompt · Visual Style Constraints

When generating a video prompt, you must inject the following visual style tags:

| Mode | Style tag |
|------|----------|
| **Universal multi-reference mode (English)** | `3D anime render, cel-shaded 3D, cinematic lighting, warm tones, high-detail textures, clear outlines` |
| **Universal first/last-frame mode (English)** | `3D anime render, cel-shaded 3D, cinematic lighting, warm tones, high-detail textures, clear outlines, shallow depth of field` |
| **Seedance 2.0 (English)** | `3D animation render, cel-shaded texture, cinematic light and shadow, warm tones, high-detail materials, clear outlines` |
