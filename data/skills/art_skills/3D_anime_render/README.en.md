# 3D Anime Render Style

This style is built specifically for the "3D anime render" theme. All art prompts, specifications, and generated content are strictly limited to:

- **3D anime world**: Set against a modern urban backdrop, covering streets, cafes, homes, offices, malls, etc., conveying the atmosphere of modern urban life.
- **Cel-shaded texture**: All characters, scenes, and props follow 3D anime rendering as the standard, emphasizing outline clarity, material delicacy, and layered light-and-shadow.
- **Healing aesthetic**: Building on 3D rendering, the style pursues a cinematic mood — warm, bright color tones, rich detail, and an overall warm, healing, bright and moving atmosphere.
- **Emotional expression**: Suited to gentle, healing, romantic urban love stories, emphasizing emotional expression through a modern urban aesthetic.

## Scope of Use

- AI art generation for 3D anime render themes (characters, scenes, props, storyboards, etc.)
- Visual work requiring an authentic 3D render texture, delicate material, and a warm, healing atmosphere

## Strictly Forbidden

- Photorealistic rendering, photo-level realism
- Dark tones, heavy shadow, excessive contrast
- Highly saturated fluorescent colors, neon colors
- Missing modern elements, dark/violent content, adult content

## Style Experience

Under this style, you will experience:

- Characters with delicate faces and a gentle or clear temperament; clothing, makeup, and hairstyles all in the 3D anime render style
- Scenes with rich spatial layering, clean and fluid outlines, and an atmosphere that is authentic and poetic
- Props, everyday objects, and environmental details all in modern form, finely crafted with a delicate texture
- Storyboards and frames both built on 3D rendering, emphasizing narrative and emotional expression

---

This style applies only to "3D anime render" related projects. For other styles (such as realistic 3D, CG, illustration, or contemporary styles), please do not use this prompt set.
