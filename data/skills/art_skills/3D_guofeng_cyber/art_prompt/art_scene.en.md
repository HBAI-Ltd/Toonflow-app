# Guofeng Cyber 3D Scene Image Generation · Constraint Manual

---

## 1. Scene aesthetic principles

1. **Spatial narrative** — the scene carries the emotion and narrative function of Eastern futurism, with traditional Chinese aesthetics as the core and cyberpunk technology as the extension; it must support both small-scale ancient-style spatial narrative and large-scale urban jianghu narrative, and is never a plain backdrop
2. **Layered depth** — every scene must have a three-layer foreground/midground/background progression: small-scale ancient-style scenes build the layers with cyber devices/holographic projections in the foreground, the core Guofeng architecture in the midground and mountains, water and mist in the background; large-scale urban scenes build volumetric depth with streets/ancient buildings in the foreground, skyscrapers in the midground and a floating city/electronic mist in the background. A flat frame is ruled out absolutely
3. **Old and new coexisting** — ancient-style elements and cyber-urban elements must fuse organically, keeping the Eastern spatial ritual order, architectural forms and aesthetic core, with cyber technology serving as an upgrade in service of the Guofeng core; stiff pile-up and stylistic rupture are ruled out. It must at the same time support all three scene classes: pure ancient-style cyberized, pure Guofeng cyber city, and ancient-style + urban fusion
4. **Texture above all** — the grain of traditional materials (wood/stone/fabric/water surfaces) and of cyber materials (anodized aluminium/carbon fibre/holographic light film/neon tubes/tempered glass/electronic screens) must be 8K ultra-clear, with PBR physical-level texture you can almost touch and tell apart, meeting both the warm feel of the ancient style and the industrial feel of the city
5. **3D as the anchor** — every frame takes AAA-game-level 3D rendering as the absolute standard; flat textures, poor CG and anime flat-color texture are rejected. RTX path tracing, global illumination, volumetric light, ambient occlusion, depth-of-field blur and other cinema-level offline render effects are mandatory, anchoring the realistic 3D style of Eastern cyberpunk while supporting both the soft lighting of the ancient style and the high-contrast lighting of the city

## 5. Four-view sheet specification

### View definitions

> The camera is fixed at the center point of the Guofeng cyber scene — small-scale ancient-style scenes use eye-level height, large-scale urban scenes may use a fixed high-altitude center point — shooting level in the four directions front/back/left/right in turn, forming a 360° all-round view with no blind spot, showing the scene's spatial structure and the layout of its cyber elements in full, supporting both the small-scale ancient-style space and the large-scale urban space.

| Position | View | View direction | Requirement | Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Top left | Front view | Level forward from the center point (0°) | Shows in full the front main structure of the Guofeng cyber scene, the foreground/midground/background depth layers, the core cyber devices and the Chinese architecture/city skyline | front view、eye level/high-altitude fixed viewpoint、looking forward、cyberpunk guofeng scene main structure |
| Top right | Right view | Level rightward from the center point (90°) | Shows in full the rightward spatial extension of the Guofeng cyber scene, the side structure of the buildings, the cyber device layout and the spatial depth / the right-side city skyline | right side view、eye level/high-altitude fixed viewpoint、looking right、cyberpunk guofeng scene side structure |
| Bottom left | Back view | Level backward from the center point (180°) | Shows in full the rear structure of the Guofeng cyber scene, the background spatial layering, the rear layout of the cyber elements and the spatial logic / the rear city skyline | back view、eye level/high-altitude fixed viewpoint、looking backward、cyberpunk guofeng scene back structure |
| Bottom right | Left view | Level leftward from the center point (270°) | Shows in full the leftward spatial extension of the Guofeng cyber scene, the side structure of the buildings, the cyber device layout and the spatial depth / the left-side city skyline | left side view、eye level/high-altitude fixed viewpoint、looking left、cyberpunk guofeng scene side structure |

### Frame specification

| Item | Constraint |
|---|---|
| Layout | A 2×2 grid evenly arranged in one frame: top row (front view + right view), bottom row (back view + left view), forming the complete four-direction view looking around from the scene's center point, with no deformation and no cropping |
| People | **Any person, human shadow, human outline or human silhouette is strictly forbidden; no living subject of any kind** |
| Viewpoint | All four views start from the same center point of the scene, with strictly identical sightline height — small-scale scenes use eye-level height, large-scale urban scenes use a uniform high-altitude viewpoint — with no vertical offset and no near/far zoom |
| Consistency | Across the four views the Chinese architectural structure, cyber device layout, neon light positions, holographic projection content, city skyline, material grain, tone and lighting, season and weather must be 100% identical, with self-consistent spatial logic |
| Light | The light direction is strictly uniform across the four views, the lighting logic of natural light + cyber artificial light is completely identical, and the position of the light sources, the light intensity and the shadow directions under the different viewpoints all obey physical rules exactly |
| Aspect ratio | 1:1 square frame, mandatory (to fit the even 2×2 grid) |

## 7. Constraint rules

### Mandatory

| No. | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | Every scene must have "a three-layer foreground/midground/background progression" with clear spatial logic; both small-scale ancient-style scenes and large-scale urban scenes must follow this strictly, and a flat frame is ruled out |
| R2 | Exterior scenes must include "aerial perspective": ancient-style scenes follow the near-ground / mid-ground / distant-mountain perspective logic, urban scenes follow the high-low perspective logic of near-ground street / mid-ground towers / distant skyline, strictly following the rule of sharp near and soft far |
| R3 | Every scene image must be a "four-view sheet" (looking around 360° from the scene's center point: front view + right view + back view + left view) evenly arranged in a 2×2 grid, supporting both eye level for small-scale scenes and a fixed high-altitude viewpoint for large-scale urban scenes |
| R4 | Across the four views the Chinese architectural structure / cyber device layout / neon light / holographic projection / city skyline / material / tone / light / season / weather must be 100% identical, with self-consistent spatial logic |
| R5 | In scene images, **any person, human shadow, human silhouette, human outline or any living subject is strictly forbidden** |
| R6 | Must include the core Guofeng Cyber 3D render keywords (at least 3 of 3D rendered / volumetric lighting / PBR materials / RTX path tracing / cyberpunk guofeng) |
| R7 | Must include cinema-level lens optical characteristics (at least one of depth of field / lens vignette / neon bokeh) |
| R8 | Every material must carry natural wear / marks of years / traces of use on cyber devices; the brand-new flawless "plastic CG feel" is forbidden, PBR physical-level texture is required, and both the warm feel of the ancient style and the industrial feel of the city must be met |
| R9 | Must follow the core principle of "old and new coexisting": ancient-style elements and cyber-urban elements must fuse organically, keeping the Eastern aesthetic core, with stiff pile-up and stylistic rupture ruled out |

### Forbidden

| No. | Forbidden |
|---|---|
| X1 | A pure white/pure black background, no effective scene content, no spatial structure |
| X2 | Extreme weather (storm/lightning/blizzard, unless the plot explicitly calls for it), or weather that contradicts the season setting |
| X3 | A scene with no depth, no layering, no separation of fore and background; a flat frame with no sense of space; an urban scene with no high-low layering |
| X4 | Vegetation/weather/tone in serious contradiction with the set season, breaking the seasonal narrative logic |
| X5 | Any person, human shadow, human silhouette, human outline or any living subject appearing |
| X6 | Architectural structure / cyber devices / neon light / city skyline / material / tone / lighting inconsistent between the four views, or a viewpoint center that is not uniform, or muddled spatial logic |
| X7 | Low-precision modeling/rough textures/plastic texture/flat coloring/poor CG effects; wording such as low-poly, rough modeling or flat color is banned |
| X8 | Materials that are too clean and perfect, with no trace of use or years, producing a strong "plastic feel" or "toy feel" |
| X9 | Lighting that is too even and flat, with no volumetric light, no ambient occlusion, no depth-of-field blur, no lens optical character, and no layering in the light |
| X10 | Stiff pile-up of Chinese traditional forms and cyber-urban elements with a stylistic rupture, or purely Western cyberpunk spliced onto Guofeng elements with no logic |
| X11 | An urban scene with no Eastern core that merely piles up Western cyber elements and loses the Guofeng aesthetic core |
| X12 | An ancient-style scene with no cyberization logic that merely forces neon onto traditional ancient buildings with no organic fusion |
