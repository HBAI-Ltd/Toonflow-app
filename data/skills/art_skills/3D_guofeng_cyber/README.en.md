# Guofeng Cyber 3D Style



######Douyin account anyexiaoshu27 — development contribution
This preset is a deep customization built on the Guofeng 3D core specification. It fuses the core of traditional Chinese Eastern aesthetics ("Guofeng") with cyberpunk sci-fi elements ("cyber") through localized, deep integration — retaining the original's core strengths of high-precision 3D rendering, traditional color palette, Eastern mood, and cinema-level lighting, while completing a Guofeng-native adaptation of cyber elements. It avoids any stiff, forced mash-up, and is compatible with the full range of AI art generation tools.

---

## I. Core Style Definition (underlying generation logic)

All art prompts, specifications, and generated content for this style are strictly limited to:

- **Guofeng Cyber 3D rendering**: Centered on 3D modeling and rendering technology, deeply fusing traditional Chinese Eastern aesthetics with the cyberpunk sci-fi core — localizing the blend of Chinese mortise-and-tenon joinery, ink-wash mood, traditional motifs, and ancient-style forms with holographic light, mechanical structure, neon light effects, and future-tech elements. Elements without a coherent core are rejected outright.
- **Ultra-fine 3D texture**: 8K high-precision models, 4K ultra-HD PBR physical material rendering, next-gen game-level topology, with metal/fabric/jade/holographic material texture pushed to the max — every detail crisp, every texture clearly discernible.
- **Exclusive Guofeng Cyber palette**: Built on a base of qinglü green, vermilion red, indigo blue, gilt gold, and ink black, paired with low-saturation Chinese-style neon (cinnabar-red neon, malachite-blue hologram, gilt glow) — no fluorescent color bleed, with elegance and futurism both present.
- **Eastern cyber aesthetic atmosphere**: An ink-wash mood where the real and the suggested interweave, layered with cyber light-and-shadow; negative-space composition; Chinese symmetrical aesthetics; misty volumetric light interlacing with holographic projection — poetic space fused perfectly with a sense of future metropolis.
- **Guofeng Cyber character styling**: Next-gen 3D ancient-style modeling with classical Eastern bone structure; costume in Hanfu form with cyber-mechanical modification; classical elegant makeup fused with cyber light-effect facial markings; carefully considered hairstyle paired with mechanical hair ornaments — Eastern charm preserved throughout.
- **Top-tier cinema-level lighting**: Top-tier UE5 / Blender Cycles rendering — volumetric light, ambient occlusion, global illumination, ray tracing, depth-of-field blur, motion blur, LUT color grading, film/TV-level cinematography, atmosphere pushed to the max.

### Scope of Use

- AI art generation for Guofeng Cyber 3D themes (character art, scene concepts, prop design, storyboard scripts, game CG, film/TV posters, etc.)
- Visual work requiring a next-gen 3D texture, an Eastern aesthetic core, and a cyber sci-fi atmosphere
- Commercial applications such as Guofeng Cyber game art, film/TV concept design, animated shorts, and digital collectibles

### Strictly Forbidden

- Realistic photography / photo-level real-person realism (unless specifically noted)
- Pure Western cyberpunk / Western fantasy / Gothic / Victorian style, or a stiff mash-up with no Guofeng core
- Everyday modern urban elements / purely sci-fi machinery with no Chinese elements
- Low-precision models / crude topology / blurry textures / clipping / malformed structure
- Excessive saturation / fluorescent color bleed / heavy digital noise / low-contrast hazy image
- Western-modeled faces or malformed bodies that violate classical Eastern bone structure / exaggerated, jarring body proportions
- Vulgar exposure / mangled costume that departs from Hanfu form / element stacking with no cultural core
