# Storyboard Prompt · Guofeng Cyber 3D · Style-Specific Techniques

## Scope of use
This Skill is used solely for generating storyboard prompts in the **Guofeng Cyber 3D (neo-Chinese cyberpunk)** style. Its core is the organic fusion expressed as "Chinese aesthetics for the soul, cyber sci-fi for the form", and it **fits both creative directions perfectly at once**:
1.  Cyberizing traditional ancient-style scenes (courts, gardens, wuxia, sects and other natively ancient-style settings)
2.  Guofeng-cyberizing modern urban scenes (shopping districts, office towers, apartments, night markets and other modern urban settings)
Stiff splicing of elements is refused; the core of Chinese aesthetics is held to.

## Fixed style anchor words (fits both ancient-style and urban · every output must contain them)

**3D render anchoring (mandatory, must come first in every output):**
3D render style, high-precision high-poly modeling, PBR physical materials, Guofeng Cyber 3D, neo-Chinese cyberpunk, Eastern sci-fi aesthetics, cinema-level lighting, ray tracing, global illumination, 8K ultra HD

**Character texture (mandatory when the shot contains a character, immediately after the render anchoring):**
Guofeng Cyber 3D character modeling, high-precision PBR textures, clear neo-Chinese costume texture, mechanical prosthetics/glowing rune detail pushed to the max, delicate physical hair rendering, real skin texture, translucent texture on neon accessories/holographic props, richly layered light and shadow, unified ancient-style/urban styling

**Scene texture (mandatory when the shot contains a scene, immediately after the render anchoring):**
Guofeng Cyber 3D scene rendering, the core of Chinese aesthetics organically fused with the scene that carries it, rich architectural/spatial detail, real texture on holographic projection/neon light effects, accurate physical texture of metal/wood/stone/glass materials, depth-of-field blur, volumetric light, ambient occlusion, physical light effects of rain curtain/mist, unified ancient-style/urban scene style

**Consistency anchoring (mandatory in reference-image mode, placed before the core logic at the end of the prompt):**
Keep the character styling, facial features and costume/prosthetic detail exactly consistent with the reference image, keep the Guofeng-cyber fusion style of the scene consistent with the reference image, keep the lighting and color keynote unified with the reference image, do not alter the core settings on your own

**Style ending (fixed, every output must contain it, placed before the image-quality lock words):**
Guofeng Cyber 3D rendering, Eastern sci-fi aesthetics, neo-Chinese cyberpunk core, Chinese aesthetics organically fused with cyber elements, unified ancient-style/urban scene style, PBR physical materials, cinema-release-level rendering

**Image-quality lock words (every output must contain them, placed after the style ending):**

## Aesthetic prohibitions (avoid strictly when generating · revised for both scene types)

The following words/styles must not appear in the output prompt:

- ❌ Photorealistic photography/photo-level realism words (such as: photorealistic, realistic photography)
- ❌ Chaotic highly saturated fluorescent colors/disordered neon with no Chinese color logic/cheap digital feel
- ❌ Purely Western cyberpunk/Japanese cyber/pure sci-fi elements with no Guofeng core
- ❌ Low-precision modeling/crude textures/plastic texture/materials with no physical properties
- ❌ Cartoon/anime/2D-otaku flattened style
- ❌ Flat design/no 3D sense of depth/wrong perspective/wrong lighting logic
- ❌ Purely Western modern architecture with no Guofeng core/purely mechanical architecture with no Chinese structure/stiff splicing of Guofeng and cyber elements
- ❌ Hollow content that loses the core of Chinese aesthetics and merely piles up cyber elements
- ❌ Purely Western modern costume with no Chinese design/pure sci-fi prosthetics with no Guofeng core
- ❌ Vulgar, cluttered, disordered visual elements that clash with the Chinese aesthetic keynote

> 💡 **Exception**: modern 3D rendering techniques (such as ray tracing, volumetric light, global illumination, physical materials) may be used normally; modern urban architecture with a Guofeng core, neo-Chinese costume and modern settings as carriers may all be used normally. At the core you must hold to the fusion keynote of "Chinese aesthetics for the soul, cyber sci-fi for the form".
