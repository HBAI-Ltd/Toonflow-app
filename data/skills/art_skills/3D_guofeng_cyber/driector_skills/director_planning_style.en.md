# Guofeng Cyber 3D Constraints · Guofeng Cyber 3D · Technique Reference
(fully adapted to both ancient-style and urban scenes)

---

## 1. Tonal system and visual keynote
(two-scene self-check: both ancient-style and urban scenes have explicit execution standards, no adaptation blind spots)

- **Tonal base** — the whole film uses moon white (C1), blue-green (C2) and indigo (C4) as its traditional Eastern base colors, overlaid with Guofeng-adapted cyber core colors (vermilion neon, blue-green fluorescence, indigo cyber light, gilt holographic color); the overall color temperature is neutral leaning cool (4500-5800K), the traditional base runs medium-high saturation (55-75%), and the cyber overlay colors may be pushed higher under control (70-90%). 【Ancient-style scenes】use the base colors as the core colors of architecture, costume, hair and props; 【urban scenes】use the same color family as the base colors of skyscraper facades, cityscape, public space, costume, hair and props, with the cyber overlay colors anchored uniformly to the traditional Eastern color system, ruling out cluttered urban neon with no Guofeng anchor.
- **Emotion-palette driven** — six emotion palettes shared by both scene types correspond to different narrative passages; each palette is set up as a two-track system of "traditional base layer + cyber overlay layer", and palette switches must track the story arc exactly:
  1. Courtly opulence/urban center of power
  2. Landscape mood/urban sky garden
  3. Boudoir gentleness/private urban residence
  4. Wuxia grimness/urban workplace confrontation/criminal-investigation gambit
  5. Festive celebration/urban street-life festival/nightlife narrative
  6. Moonlit night stillness/urban night-view solitude/city scene-only shots
- **Warm/cool narrative contrast** — traditional warm colors (vermilion C3, golden yellow C5, rouge C7, gamboge C9) + cyber warm light (neon vermilion, holographic gilt) act as the visual signal of a narrative turn, used for emotional warming and festive passages; traditional cool colors (indigo C4, ink black C6) + cyber cool light (fluorescent blue-green, cyber indigo) are used for grim, melancholy and secluded passages; switching the cyber light on/off or changing its color can serve as the visual anchor of a strong narrative turn. In 【ancient-style scenes】traditional colors lead and cyber colors follow; in 【urban scenes】the weight of the two tracks may be balanced as the narrative requires.
- **Palette-first principle** — passage planning must bind an emotional scene first, then settle the "traditional main color + secondary color + cyber overlay color" and the lighting scheme, to avoid "the plot is right but the emotion is in the wrong color", and to keep the color logic of ancient-style and urban scenes completely unified.
- **Banned color range** — highly saturated cluttered fluorescent colors with no Guofeng adaptation, jarring modern digital colors, cheap low-quality neon colors and illogical Western-cyberpunk color clashes are all incompatible with this style; 【urban scenes】additionally ban: jumpy street-advertising colors with no Guofeng anchor, cluttered street-life neon, and purely industrial cold-gray palettes.

## 2. Lighting scheme system
(two-scene self-check: all 7 schemes fit both ancient-style and urban, each states its use in both scene types, no execution blind spots)

- **Lighting is narrative** — the 7 two-track Guofeng-cyber lighting schemes correspond to different emotional passages; in the planning stage the director should set the keynote direction of "traditional base light + cyber overlay light" at passage level rather than assigning it shot by shot without logic, and ancient-style and urban scenes can reuse the same scheme framework directly.
- **Core 3D render lighting characteristics** — volumetric light, ambient occlusion (AO) and depth-of-field blur are the core lighting devices of the Guofeng base layer, with holographic light effects, neon rim light, cyber screen-surface reflection and energy volumetric light added as the core lighting devices of the cyber layer; 【urban scenes】additionally adapt: neon rim light on building bodies, multi-layer reflection on glass curtain walls, volumetric light in urban-canyon streets and alleys, area light from holographic advertising, backlight along the skyscraper skyline, and numerically controlled light sources in underground spaces. Every lighting scheme must express the texture of PBR physical material rendering, keeping the lighting logic of the traditional layer and the cyber layer unified and physically credible.

| Lighting scheme | Scheme name | Tonal tendency | Applicable emotion and scenes in both scene types |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Cyber warm-light splendor | Vermilion traditional base + golden yellow holographic highlights + moon white base + vermilion neon rim light | 【Ancient-style scenes】courtly opulence, solemn grandeur, festive scale, power narrative<br>【Urban scenes】top-floor power space, skyscraper corporate headquarters, city-landmark celebration, core narrative climax |
| B | Holographic blue-green mood | Blue-green traditional base + moon white misty volumetric light + blue-green fluorescent diffusion + holographic landscape light | 【Ancient-style scenes】landscape mood, poetic distance, ethereal grace, cyber zen<br>【Urban scenes】Guofeng sky garden, urban ecological landmark, holographic landscape scenery, zen office space |
| C | Numerically controlled soft light, warm shadow | Rouge warm-tone base + golden yellow accents + soft shadow + warm-tone holographic atmosphere light | 【Ancient-style scenes】boudoir gentleness, tender delicacy, everyday warmth, private narrative<br>【Urban scenes】high-end private residence, everyday domestic scenes, tender emotional passages, solitude narrative |
| D | Prosthetic cool-toned grimness | Ink black traditional base + indigo hard light + high-contrast light-dark cutting + cool cyber energy light | 【Ancient-style scenes】wuxia grimness, austere sharpness, oppressive atmosphere, conflict and confrontation<br>【Urban scenes】workplace gambit, criminal-investigation confrontation, underground-space narrative, high-tension conflict passages |
| E | Numerically controlled diffusion through window gauze | Moon white base + natural side light + ambient occlusion + window-lattice holographic projection light | 【Ancient-style scenes】interior daytime, everyday domestic life, quiet refinement, everyday narrative<br>【Urban scenes】daytime office space, urban residence in daytime, still spaces such as tea rooms/studies |
| F | Cyber clear light of a moonlit night | Indigo traditional base + moon white cool light + golden yellow warm accents + artificial-moonlight holographic light | 【Ancient-style scenes】moonlit night stillness, serene beauty, longing and solitude, atmosphere-led narrative<br>【Urban scenes】city night view, skyscraper night-view scene-only shots, night shooting in streets and alleys, solitary emotional passages |
| G | Neon festival warm light | Vermilion traditional base + gamboge warm light + highly saturated Guofeng neon light + holographic festival projection light | 【Ancient-style scenes】festival celebration, lively joy, street-life warmth, ensemble narrative<br>【Urban scenes】urban festival, Guofeng street-life night market, city nightlife, lively ensemble passages |

- **Warm/cool light allocation** — traditional warm light (vermilion/golden yellow/gamboge) + cyber warm light (neon vermilion/holographic gilt) suits opulent, gentle and festive passages; traditional cool light (indigo/ink black) + cyber cool light (fluorescent blue-green/cyber indigo) suits grim, melancholy and secluded passages. The director may adjust the warm/cool switch points as the narrative requires, and the cyber light going on or off can serve as the visual signal of a strong narrative beat; ancient-style and urban scenes follow the same warm/cool logic.
- **Atmosphere direction mapping** — the atmosphere direction of every scene should map onto one of the lighting schemes above (A-G), keeping the visual logic of the traditional layer and the cyber layer completely consistent and the style unified throughout in ancient-style and urban scenes alike.

## 3. Texture direction
(two-scene self-check: full material coverage for ancient-style and urban, explicit standards for the texture of years in both scene types, a plastic feel/CG feel ruled out)

- **3D rendering is the two-track anchor** — the core of Guofeng Cyber 3D: high-precision two-track modeling, PBR physical material rendering, volumetric light in the traditional layer + holographic light in the cyber layer, ambient occlusion and depth-of-field blur, delivering a cinema-level 3D rendered frame throughout. 【Ancient-style scenes】focus high-precision modeling on ancient architecture, traditional costume/hair/props and natural landscape; 【urban scenes】focus high-precision two-track modeling on neo-Chinese skyscrapers, urban public space, Guofeng cyber vehicles and urban costume/hair/props. Both scene types follow the same PBR rendering standard strictly.
- **Two-track PBR material fusion** — every costume/hair/prop and scene asset must be made credible through PBR physical rendering, with "organic fusion of traditional materials and cyber materials" at its core, covering both scene types:
  - 【Ancient-style core materials】the sheen and drape of silk, the grain and patina of wood, the reflection and feel of metal, the translucence and warmth of jade, the glaze sheen of porcelain
  - 【Urban core materials】the clear reflection of the glass curtain wall against the light-and-shadow cutting of Chinese lattice, the texture of reinforced concrete fused with the grain of traditional stone, the matte feel of aluminum panels against the aged reflection of traditional bronze fittings, the wear marks of asphalt paving echoing the weathering grain of bluestone slabs, the flowing light of optical-fiber fabric unified with the drape of traditional silk
  - Every fused material must be self-consistent in physical logic; meaningless piling up of cyber materials is forbidden.
- **Two-track reinforcement of volumetric light and depth of field** — volumetric light is the soul of the Guofeng-cyber frame: 【ancient-style scenes】exterior scenes must have atmospheric perspective and the double volumetric-light scattering of traditional daylight + cyber neon, while interior scenes create multi-layer volumetric light through window light/candlelight/holographic projection; 【urban scenes】exteriors must have volumetric light in urban-canyon streets and alleys, daylight diffusion across the skyscraper cluster, and volumetric scattering of neon light, while interior scenes create multi-layer volumetric light through window-lattice projection/holographic screens/numerically controlled light sources. Depth-of-field blur reinforces the spatial depth of both scene types in step, adapting to the different spatial scales of ancient-style and urban settings.
- **Two-track unity in the texture of years** — materials must not be too clean and perfect; both scene types must show marks of living and the feel of years:
  - 【Ancient-style scenes】wood surfaces carry marks of use, stone surfaces carry weathering grain and moss, cloth carries natural creases, roof tiles carry moss traces and chips
  - 【Urban scenes】asphalt paving carries rut wear, glass curtain walls carry water stains and weathering marks, metal facades carry oxidation and scratches, street fixtures carry marks of use, neon signs carry local strobing defects
  - Throughout, a "plastic feel", a brand-new flawless "cheap CG feel" and a "show-flat" urban frame with no marks of living are forbidden.
- **Cyber does not mean cold** — Guofeng Cyber 3D stresses the balance between the warmth of Eastern aesthetics and the avant-garde edge of cyber sci-fi, conveying emotion through two-track material texture, layered lighting and traditional + cyber fused color pairing rather than relying on meaningless spectacle effects and violent piling up of cyber elements; the core of Eastern aesthetics must be held to in ancient-style and urban scenes alike.

## 4. Guofeng cyber scene spatial elements
(two-scene self-check: every element fits both scene types, urban-specific Guofeng cyber elements added, full coverage of ancient-style and urban narrative function)

The two-track scene elements particular to the ancient-style cyber world and their visual narrative function, with "traditional Eastern spatial logic organically fused with cyber sci-fi elements" at the core, **100% adapted to traditional ancient-style scenes and Guofeng cyber urban scenes**; illogical piling up of elements is forbidden:

- **Framing composition elements** — 【ancient-style scenes】gauze curtain/screen/Chinese door frame; 【urban scenes】holographic gauze-curtain partition/numerically controlled Chinese screen/AR Chinese door frame/semi-transparent numerically controlled glass curtain wall. Natural framing props for composition, creating the layering and spatial depth of "the real and the suggested giving rise to each other". In a 3D render, the way light passes through semi-transparent materials, the switching of holographic projection between real and suggested, and the narrative boundary cut by the frame are the highlights of the image; they can be used for real/illusory transitions, memory flashbacks and identity metaphors in the narrative.
- **Negative-space mood elements** — 【ancient-style scenes】Chinese courtyard/traditional flowering trees/natural curtain of rain/wind through a bamboo grove; 【urban scenes】Guofeng sky garden/rooftop holographic flowering trees/vertical greening landscape/numerically controlled curtain of rain/rainy street-and-alley view/sea of clouds between skyscrapers. Natural vehicles for negative-space composition, where the scene is the feeling: a courtyard full of blossom = release, sitting alone in the rain = solitude, leaves falling = the sorrow of parting. In a 3D scene, the volume of the vegetation and its interplay with the light, the refraction of light through the curtain of rain, and the overlay of real and suggested scenes matter especially.
- **Light-carrying elements** — 【ancient-style scenes】candle fire/natural moonlight/wooden-lattice window light/lantern; 【urban scenes】holographic Chinese candle fire/artificial cyber moonlight/numerically controlled Chinese window-lattice projection light/skyscraper window-lattice area light/Guofeng neon lantern. The two-track light-carrying vehicles of the Guofeng cyber world, with traditional and cyber sources paired one to one: candlelight/holographic candle fire = warm/private (scheme C), moonlight/artificial cyber moonlight = cool/secluded (scheme F), window light/numerically controlled window-lattice light = everyday/still (scheme E). In a 3D render, the double volumetric-light effect of the source and the traditional + cyber double reflection of PBR materials are the key to the texture of the image.
- **Signature architectural elements** — 【ancient-style scenes】traditional flying eaves/wooden dougong brackets/green tiled roofs/mortise-and-tenon structure; 【urban scenes】neo-Chinese skyscraper flying eaves/steel-structure mechanical dougong/facades with Chinese elements/metal roofs with green-tile grain/mortise-and-tenon steel structure. The signature elements of Guofeng cyber architecture; the 3D modeling must express in step the refined detail of traditional carved beams and painted rafters and the industrial beauty of cyber mechanical structure, and the materials must present in step the marks of years in the traditional layer and the wear and oxidation of the cyber layer. Illogical mechanical modification that departs from ancient architectural form is forbidden.
- **Core street-life narrative elements** — 【ancient-style scenes】old streets and alleys/street-life shops/memorial archway; 【urban scenes】Guofeng cyber streets and alleys/skyscraper canyon/neo-Chinese street-life shops/streets and alleys of Guofeng neon signs/holographic Chinese ceremonial gate. The core spatial vehicles of street-life narrative in both scene types; the 3D render must express the spatial depth of the streets and alleys, the unity of the Guofeng elements and the lived feel of street-life warmth. These are the core settings for ensemble narrative and everyday passages.
- **Passage transition rule** — this style has a rich stock of scene assets for both scene types (variants across times of day/weather/seasons/degrees of cyberization), so use scene-only shots as an emotional buffer between passages rather than cutting hard. 【Ancient-style scenes】fit courtyard/landscape/architecture scene-only shots; 【urban scenes】fit skyscraper skyline/sky garden/city landmark/street-and-alley/subway/skybridge scene-only shots.
- **Narrative priority rule** — deliver turning points through visuals rather than dialogue, preferring visual means (an abrupt light change, a shot-size jump cut, a real/illusory scene-only-shot metaphor, a switch of holographic projection content, cyber elements going on or off) over relying on dialogue to explain; ancient-style and urban scenes follow the same narrative logic.

## 5. Guofeng cyber instruments and ambient sound
(two-scene self-check: full coverage of the sound system in both scene types, explicit adaptation for both ancient-style and urban emotions, nothing jarring)

Constraints on the two-track sound elements of the Guofeng cyber world, with "the core of traditional Eastern instruments organically fused with cyber electronic music" at its center, **adapted in step to ancient-style scenes and Guofeng cyber urban scenes**; jarring piling up of electronic music is forbidden:

### Instrument choice
(shared by both scene types, directly reusable in ancient-style and urban narrative alike)
- **Xiao (vertical bamboo flute)** — the core instrument for desolate, solitary and grieving passages; can be paired with an electronic delay effect to strengthen the cold, plaintive cyber atmosphere; best able to express cold, plaintive sorrow
- **Erhu** — for passages of surging emotion, grief and longing; the crying quality of the bowed strings suits an emotional outburst; can be paired with a distortion effect to strengthen the cyber narrative tension of fate
- **Suona** — for passages of violent emotional swing (great grief or great joy, a turn of fate, a climax); use sparingly, but it lands like a bomb; can be paired with a synthesizer noise floor to strengthen the extreme emotional impact of the cyber world
- **Guqin** — setting the key at the opening / steady passages; used together with the xiao, expressing the landscape mood; can be paired with an ambient electronic pad to strengthen the ethereal quality of cyber zen
- **Pipa** — an accent for tense, urgent passages; suits wuxia grimness/urban gambit scenes; can be paired with an industrial electronic drum pattern to strengthen the sharp sense of rhythm
- **Guzheng** — the atmosphere instrument for courtly opulence/urban festival passages, ornate and graceful; can be paired with an electronic synthesizer arpeggio to strengthen the grand atmosphere
- Strings underneath can add cinematic feeling but must not upstage the rest; electronic synthesizer pads must be anchored to the tonality of the traditional instruments, and illogical electronic sound effects that depart from the Guofeng core are forbidden

### Instrument combination strategy
(fully adapted to both scene types; every combination states the ancient-style/urban scenes it applies to)

| Emotional stage | Instrument combination | Notes on adaptation to both scene types |
|---|---|---|
| Steady/opening/ending | Solo guqin, or guqin + xiao + a light ambient electronic pad | 【Ancient-style scenes】landscape/courtyard opening, ending of the whole film<br>【Urban scenes】city wide shot (全景)/skyline opening, everyday commuting, ending of the whole film |
| Landscape mood/ethereal | Xiao + guqin + dizi + synthesizer ambience | 【Ancient-style scenes】landscape travel, zen passages, scene-only-shot narrative<br>【Urban scenes】sky garden, urban ecological landscape, zen-space passages |
| Courtly opulence/festive | Guzheng + bianzhong + strings + electronic arpeggio accents | 【Ancient-style scenes】court narrative, festival celebration, grand ceremony<br>【Urban scenes】top-floor power narrative, urban festival, city grand ceremony, moments of glory |
| Grief deepening | Xiao + erhu + delay effect + low-saturation electronic noise floor | 【Ancient-style scenes】parting, longing, passages of deepening grief<br>【Urban scenes】urban solitude, passages of disappointment, narrative of sinking emotion |
| Emotional outburst/turn of fate | Solo suona, or suona + erhu + distorted synthesizer | 【Ancient-style scenes】parting in life or death, a turn of fate, the climax of the whole film<br>【Urban scenes】a turning point in life, extreme conflict, the core climax of the whole film |
| Wuxia grimness/tension | Pipa accents + strings underneath + industrial electronic drum pattern | 【Ancient-style scenes】wuxia confrontation, pursuit and gambit, tense chase passages<br>【Urban scenes】workplace gambit, criminal-investigation confrontation, high-tension conflict passages |
| Gentle everyday | Guqin + dizi + light strings + minimal electronic white noise | 【Ancient-style scenes】domestic routine, boudoir narrative, warm everyday passages<br>【Urban scenes】urban domestic life, office routine, tender everyday narrative |

### Guofeng cyber ambient sound
(designed in layers for both scene types; ancient-style and urban scenes each have explicit execution standards)

- **Base ambient-sound layer shared by both scene types** — cicadas and insects / a babbling brook / wind through a bamboo grove / Guofeng street-vendor calls / night rain dripping from the eaves / the rustle of cloth / the light ring of a Chinese wind chime / birdsong / blossom falling with a whisper
- **Layering of ambient sound specific to the Guofeng cyber city** — 【traditional layer】folk-music background on the street, wind through Chinese lattice, the wind-chime sound of Guofeng signage, street-vendor calls; 【cyber layer】wind noise around skyscrapers, the low hum of traffic, the low-frequency sound of subway operation, the buzz of neon signs, faint voices from holographic advertising, the light sounds of numerically controlled equipment, wind noise at the glass curtain wall
- **Execution standard** — 【ancient-style scenes】annotate 1-2 core traditional ambient sounds per scene; 【urban scenes】annotate 1 core traditional ambient sound + 1 core urban cyber ambient sound per scene, to help the later sound design. The better the layering of the two-track ambient sound matches the narrative, the stronger the immersion of the Guofeng cyber setting.
