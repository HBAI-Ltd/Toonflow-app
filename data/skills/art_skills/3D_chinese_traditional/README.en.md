# Guofeng 3D Style

This style is built specifically for the "Guofeng 3D" theme. All art prompts, specifications, and generated content are strictly limited to:

- **Guofeng 3D rendering**: Presenting traditional Chinese aesthetics ("Guofeng" — the Chinese-national aesthetic) through 3D modeling and rendering technology, covering classical architecture, landscape gardens, court dress, etc.
- **Fine 3D texture**: High-precision models, PBR material rendering, richly layered light-and-shadow, and clearly discernible detail.
- **Traditional Chinese color**: Traditional palettes such as qinglü green, vermilion red, indigo blue, and golden yellow, in harmonious, refined color.
- **Eastern aesthetic atmosphere**: Deep, evocative mood, an interplay of the real and the suggested, negative-space composition, and poetic space.
- **Guofeng character styling**: 3D Guofeng-era modeling, elaborate and refined costume, elegant makeup, and carefully considered hairstyle.
- **Cinema-level lighting**: Volumetric light, ambient occlusion, global illumination, depth-of-field blur, and other cinematic rendering effects.

## Scope of Use

- AI art generation for Guofeng 3D themes (characters, scenes, props, storyboards, etc.)
- Visual work requiring a 3D render texture, Eastern aesthetic, and Guofeng-era mood
- Game art, film/TV concept design, Guofeng animation, and other application scenarios

## Strictly Forbidden

- Realistic photography / photo-level realism (unless specifically noted)
- Western fantasy / Gothic / Victorian style
- Cyberpunk / sci-fi / modern urban elements
- Low-precision models / crude modeling / blurry textures
- Excessive saturation / fluorescent color / overly digital feel

## Style Experience

Under this style, you will experience:

- Character styling that is elegant and refined, with delicate 3D modeling, clear costume texture, and flowing hair strands
- Scenes with grand, sweeping scale, rich architectural detail, authentic material texture, and a poetic lighting atmosphere
- Carefully considered props and objects, with fine material craftsmanship and harmonious, unified color
- Storyboards and rendering both emphasizing 3D texture, Eastern mood, and cinema-level lighting
