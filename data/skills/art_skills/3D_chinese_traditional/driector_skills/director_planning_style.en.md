# Guofeng 3D Constraints · Guofeng 3D · Technique Reference

---

## 1. Tonal system and visual keynote

- **Tonal base** — the whole film uses moon white (C1), blue-green (C2) and indigo (C4) as its base colors, with an overall color temperature toward neutral (4800-5500K) and medium-high saturation (55-75%), giving the elegant, expansive tone of traditional Eastern aesthetics
- **Emotion-palette driven** — the six emotion palettes (courtly opulence/landscape mood/boudoir gentleness/wuxia grimness/festive celebration/moonlit night stillness) correspond to different narrative passages; palette switches should track the story arc
- **Warm/cool narrative contrast** — warm colors (vermilion C3, golden yellow C5, rouge C7, gamboge C9) act as the visual signal of a narrative turn, used for emotional warming and festive passages; cool colors (indigo C4, ink black C6) are used for grim, melancholy and secluded passages
- **Palette-first principle** — passage planning must bind an emotional scene first (courtly opulence/landscape mood/boudoir gentleness/wuxia grimness/festive celebration/moonlit night stillness, etc.), then settle the main + secondary colors and the lighting scheme, to avoid "the plot is right but the emotion is in the wrong color"
- **Banned color range** — highly saturated fluorescent colors, neon colors and the modern digital color system are all incompatible with this style

## 2. Lighting scheme system

- **Lighting is narrative** — the 7 lighting schemes correspond to different emotional passages; in the planning stage the director should set the lighting keynote direction at passage level rather than shot by shot
- **3D render lighting characteristics** — volumetric light, ambient occlusion (AO) and depth-of-field blur are the core lighting devices of the Guofeng 3D style; every lighting scheme must express the texture of PBR physical material rendering

| Lighting scheme | Scheme name | Tonal tendency | Applicable emotion |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Warm-light splendor | Vermilion + golden yellow highlights + moon white base | Courtly opulence, solemn grandeur, festive scale |
| B | Blue-green mood | Blue-green + moon white mist + volumetric light diffusion | Landscape mood, poetic distance, ethereal grace |
| C | Soft light, warm shadow | Rouge warm tone + golden yellow accents + soft shadow | Boudoir gentleness, tender delicacy, everyday warmth |
| D | Cool-toned grimness | Ink black + indigo + hard-light contrast | Wuxia grimness, austere sharpness, oppressive atmosphere |
| E | Diffusion through window gauze | Moon white base + natural side light + ambient occlusion | Interior daytime, everyday domestic life, quiet refinement |
| F | Clear light of a moonlit night | Indigo + moon white cool light + golden yellow warm accents | Moonlit night stillness, serene beauty, longing and solitude |
| G | Festival warm light | Vermilion + gamboge + highly saturated warm light | Festival celebration, lively joy, rich color |

- **Warm/cool light allocation** — warm light (vermilion/golden yellow/gamboge) suits opulent, gentle and festive passages; cool light (indigo/ink black) suits grim, melancholy and secluded passages. The director may adjust the warm/cool switch points as the narrative requires
- **Atmosphere direction mapping** — the atmosphere direction of every scene should map onto one of the lighting schemes above (A-G), to keep the visuals consistent

## 3. Texture direction

- **3D rendering is the anchor** — the core of Guofeng 3D: high-precision modeling, PBR material rendering, volumetric light, ambient occlusion and depth-of-field blur, delivering a cinema-level 3D rendered frame
- **PBR materials above all** — the materials of every costume, hairstyle and prop must be made credible through PBR physical rendering: the sheen and drape of silk, the grain and patina of wood, the reflection and feel of metal, the translucence and warmth of jade, the glaze sheen of porcelain
- **Volumetric light and depth of field** — volumetric light is the soul of the Guofeng 3D frame: exterior scenes must have atmospheric perspective and volumetric light scattering, interior scenes create volumetric light effects through window light/candlelight; depth-of-field blur reinforces spatial depth
- **The texture of years** — materials must not be too clean and perfect: wood surfaces carry marks of use, stone surfaces carry weathering and moss, cloth carries natural creases, roof tiles carry moss traces and chips. A "plastic feel" and a brand-new flawless "CG feel" are forbidden
- **3D does not mean cold** — Guofeng 3D emphasizes the warmth of Eastern aesthetics, conveying emotion through material texture, lighting layers and color pairing rather than relying on spectacle effects

## 4. Guofeng-era scene spatial elements

Scene elements particular to the Guofeng-era world and their visual narrative function:

- **Gauze curtain/screen/door frame** — natural framing props for composition, creating a "can't see through it" sense of layering and spatial depth. In a 3D render, the semi-transparent material of the gauze curtain and the way light passes through it are highlights of the frame
- **Courtyard/flowering trees/curtain of rain** — natural vehicles for negative-space composition, where the scene is the feeling: a courtyard full of blossom = release, sitting alone in the rain = solitude, leaves falling = the sorrow of parting. In a 3D scene, the volume of the vegetation and its interplay with the light matter especially
- **Candle fire/moonlight/window light** — the light-carrying vehicles of the Guofeng-era world: candlelight = warm/private (scheme C), moonlight = cool/secluded (scheme F), window light = everyday/still (scheme E). In a 3D render, the volumetric light effect of the light source and PBR material reflection are the key
- **Flying eaves/dougong brackets/green roof tiles** — the signature elements of Guofeng-era architecture; the 3D modeling must express the refined detail of carved beams and painted rafters, and the materials must carry the feel of years
- **Bridge passages with scene-only shots** — this style has a rich stock of scene assets (variants across times of day/weather/seasons), so use scene-only shots as an emotional buffer between passages rather than cutting hard
- **Turning points through visuals, not dialogue** — prefer visual means (an abrupt light change, a shot-size jump cut, an empty-shot metaphor) over relying on dialogue to explain

## 5. Guofeng-era instruments and ambient sound

Constraints on the sound elements of the Guofeng-era world:

### Instrument choice

- **Xiao (vertical bamboo flute)** — the core instrument for desolate, solitary and grieving passages; best able to express cold, plaintive sorrow
- **Erhu** — for passages of surging emotion, grief and longing; the crying quality of the bowed strings suits an emotional outburst
- **Suona** — for passages of violent emotional swing (great grief or great joy, a turn of fate, a climax); use sparingly, but it lands like a bomb
- **Guqin** — setting the key at the opening / steady passages; used together with the xiao, expressing the landscape mood
- **Pipa** — an accent for tense, urgent passages; suits wuxia grimness scenes
- **Guzheng** — the atmosphere instrument for courtly opulence and festival celebration passages, ornate and graceful
- Strings underneath can add cinematic feeling but must not upstage the rest

### Instrument combination strategy

| Emotional stage | Instrument combination |
|---|---|
| Steady/opening/closing | Guqin solo, or guqin + xiao |
| Landscape mood/otherworldly | Xiao + guqin + dizi flute |
| Courtly opulence/celebration | Guzheng + bianzhong chime bells + strings |
| Grief thickening | Xiao + erhu |
| Emotional outburst/turn of fate | Suona solo, or suona + erhu |
| Wuxia grimness/tension | Pipa accents + strings underneath |
| Gentle everyday life | Guqin + dizi flute + light strings |

### Guofeng-era ambient sound

- **Typical ambient sound layers** — cicadas and insects singing / a stream babbling / wind through the bamboo grove / market hawkers calling / night rain dripping from the eaves / the rustle of fabric / a wind chime ringing lightly / birds chirping / petals rustling down
- **Mark 1-2 core ambient sounds for every scene**, to help the later sound effect design. The richer the ambient sound layers, the more immersive the Guofeng-era scene
