# Global Aesthetic Baseline · Live-Action Urban

---
You must strictly and completely follow every style constraint and global rule below, and generate the prompt strictly in the prompt-template format; output only the prompt body, and do not attach any explanation, note, comment, heading or other extra text.

## 1. Style DNA

| Dimension | Definition |
|---|---|
| **Primary style** | Live-Action Urban — a contemporary Chinese urban visual system whose only imaging medium is a live-action camera |
| **Secondary style** | Urban drama-film photography aesthetics · theatrical-release-grade live-action image quality |
| **Image lineage** | The breathing of handheld camerawork, the accidents of natural light, the real presence of faces — following Wong Kar-wai's nights, Kore-eda's everyday, the Dardenne brothers' closeness, Lou Ye's urban drifting |
| **Emotional key** | Never force sentiment, never dodge fragility; find poetry in the creases of daily life, hear loneliness in the city's noise; warm but not saccharine, restrained but not cold |
| **Texture anchor words** | Visible pores on real skin, the drape and friction marks of fabric, layered space inside glass reflections, daylight striped by blinds, reflections on the ground after rain, a lived-in scene nobody arranged |

## 2. Camera grammar (replacing the "render engine")

> Live-Action Urban does not rely on a render pipeline. Image quality comes from the choice of camera, the character of the lens, the staging of light. Below are the core parameters of that "camera grammar".

### 2.1 Frame and format

| Parameter | Baseline value | Aesthetic intent |
|---|---|---|
| Aspect ratio | 1.85:1 (widescreen) or 16:9 | Lateral urban space, the coexistence of character and environment; vertical (9:16) only in a phone-narrative context |
| Image format | 35mm film texture / full-frame digital cinematography | Shallow depth of field, natural vignette falloff, subtle grain structure |
| Color science | ARRI Alexa / Sony Venice class cinema color science | Natural skin-tone reproduction, soft highlight rolloff, shadows that are not crushed and keep gradation |

### 2.2 Lens character

| Focal length | Narrative character | Typical use |
|---|---|---|
| 24-28mm (wide) | Environment swallows the character, spatial alienation, the vastness of the city against the smallness of the individual | Urban insert shots, loneliness in a crowd, the relation between architecture and people |
| 35mm (mild wide) | Subjective viewpoint, being-there, documentary feel | Street following, indoor conversation, everyday walking |
| 50mm (standard) | Equivalent to the human eye, objective gaze, neither flattering nor distorting | Character portraits, two-person dialogue, emotional gazing |
| 85mm (medium telephoto) | Compresses space, shortens emotional distance, background falls off to isolate the character | Close-up (特写), voyeuristic viewpoint, seen from far across the street |
| 135mm+ (telephoto) | Extreme compression, a sense of spying, peels the character off the environment | The isolated individual in the city, gazing into the distance, the physical distance of emotion |

### 2.3 Camera position and movement

| Technique | Visual psychology | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| Locked-off camera position (固定机位) | Calm, observing, letting time speak for itself | Everyday scenes, dialogue, insert shots as breathing space |
| Slight handheld sway | Presence, breathing, subjective involvement | Emotional swings, street walking, intimate following |
| Steadicam/gimbal | Elegant flow, the poetry of urban drifting | Major scene transitions, a character's entrance, showing a space |
| High angle | Sense of fate, smallness, the god's-eye view | Looking down on the city, the closing look back, a character trapped |
| Low angle | Oppression or grandeur | The pressure of skyscrapers, a character's strength |
| Rack focus | The shift of attention, the change of a relationship | Focus wandering while characters look at each other, revealing hidden information |

## 3. City color spectrum — the real colors of light and materials

> The colors of Live-Action Urban come from the real light and materials of Chinese cities, not from an engine palette. The palettes below are pulled from: reflections on office tower curtain walls, the years on the concrete walls of an old housing block, a rainy night under streetlamps, the fluorescence of a convenience store.

### 3.1 Urban light-source spectrum

| Light source | Color temperature (K) | Light description | Urban field |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dawn light (1h after sunrise) | 3500-4500 | Low-angle warm gold diffusion, light spread with a thin haze in the air | The empty early subway, the streets of sanitation workers, steam from a breakfast stall |
| Morning daylight | 5500-6000 | Cool white and clear, sharp shadows, building-surface materials at their most tangible | Reflections on office glass, clean glare off the street, the commuting flow |
| Midday toplight | 6000-6500 | Cold hard light, short and heavy shadows, deep shadows in the eye sockets/under the jaw | Office atriums, exposed streets — use with care |
| Golden hour (1h before sunset) | 2800-3500 | The most precious warm tone, long shadows, every material gilded at the edge | The first choice of light for all warm and memory passages |
| Blue hour (20min after sunset) | 8000-10000 | Deep blue-violet sky, city lights still weaker than the sky, cool and quietly poetic | Solitude, transitions, inner monologue, the final beat |
| High-pressure sodium lamp (old streetlight) | 2000-2200 | The signature warm orange-yellow, the body temperature of the city at night | Old-town alleys, walking alone late at night, the texture of memory |
| LED streetlight | 4000-5000 | Cool white or slightly blue, the calm face of the modern city | New districts, under elevated roads, the night around office towers |
| Convenience store/subway fluorescents | 4000-6500 | Indifferent but safe white light, the island in the late-night city | Late-night convenience store, last subway train, the duty room |
| Screen light (phone/computer/ad screen) | Variable | Cool blue or warm white frontal light lighting one cheek, the lonely companion | Moments alone, insomnia, the distance of the information age |
| Headlight beam | 3000 (halogen warm) / 6000 (LED cool) | A moving point source, volumetric effect where the beam passes through rain and mist | Street night scenes, a car chase, passing each other by |

### 3.2 Urban material spectrum

| Material | Color range | Where it sits in the city |
|---|---|---|
| Glass curtain wall | Blue-gray → deep blue → the gold/orange of the reflected sky | The CBD skyline, office towers, malls |
| Concrete/cement | Beige-gray → cool gray → deep gray after rain, with water stains and moss blotches | Facades of old housing blocks, bridges, underpasses |
| Red brick | Ochre red → dark brick red, gray-brown once weathered | Old-town dwellings, old campus buildings, creative parks |
| Asphalt road | Deep gray, near black, mirror-like reflection after rain | Every city road |
| Stainless steel/aluminum alloy | Cool silver-gray, brushed or mirror finish | Office lobbies, subway cars, bus stops |
| Street tree green | Fresh spring green → deep summer green → autumn caramel/golden yellow | Streets, parks, residential compounds |
| White wall (interior) | Warm white leaning beige (old flats) / cool white leaning blue (newly fitted) | Rented rooms, offices, hospitals |

### 3.3 Narrative-emotion palettes

> No longer a mechanical split into "primary/secondary color", but a definition of a scene's visual temperature by "the emotion of the light".

| Emotional scene | Light recipe | Color temperature direction | Visual keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| Setting out at dawn | Dawn light entering at 45° + thin-haze diffusion | Warm light over a cool base (3600K daylight + 5500K ambient skylight) | Clear, hopeful, quiet, gathering momentum |
| Workplace routine | Window light as key + cool screen light as fill | Neutral leaning cool (5000K baseline) | Order, focus, restraint, distance |
| Afternoon slack | Broad diffused window light + curtains softening it | Warm white (4500K) | Languid, healing, the poetry of the everyday |
| Ambiguous closeness | Warm-source side light (desk lamp/candle/sunset) + shadows keeping detail | Very warm (2800-3200K) | Warmth, distance dissolving, words never said |
| Street-life warmth | Mixed sources (warm streetlight + cool white shopfront + headlights sweeping past) | Warm-dominant (3000K warm + local cool counterpoint) | Lively, real, human, warmth inside the crowding |
| Walking alone on a rainy night | Wet ground reflecting streetlights + rain-curtain scatter + distant neon defocused | Cool base (6000K) + warm light spots (2800K) as accents | Loneliness, contemplation, the relation between city and individual |
| Late-night fragility | A single source (desk lamp/phone screen/streetlight outside) lighting like an island | A warm point (3000K) sinking into deep dark (pure black where there is no light) | Fragility, truth, the mask dropped, the hour of being alone |
| Confrontation | Hard side light + deep shadows + warm-cool counterpoint (cool window light on one side / warm interior on the other) | Split (cool/warm color temperatures never blending) | Tension, opposition, a relationship breaking |
| The moment of parting | Backlight (character in silhouette) + long shadows + blue-hour sky | Cool (8000K sky) + warm (2800K streetlight), a farewell coexistence of cool and warm | Reluctance, release, the end of a chapter |

## 4. Global constraint rules

### 4.1 Image-authenticity constraints (mandatory)

| No. | Rule |
|---|---|
| P1 | Must anchor generation on "live-action photography / live-action shooting / a still from a live-action film" — the image must read as "shot by a camera", not "generated by a computer" |
| P2 | Must specify one concrete light-source logic: where does the light in this scene come from? (window/lamp/sky/screen) Direction, color temperature and hardness of the source must be self-consistent |
| P3 | Must keep the real texture of the character's skin — pores, fine lines, moles, natural unevenness of tone — no plastic smoothing, no AI-looking mask of a face |
| P4 | Hair must be in a natural state — blown loose by wind, wet with rain, strands escaping a tie — no modelled, tidy blocks of hair |
| P5 | Clothing must show the traces of "having been worn" — creased fabric, slight pilling, a naturally deformed collar, worn trouser hems — no brand-new sample-garment look |
| P6 | The scene must carry evidence of "having been used" — objects scattered naturally on a desk, marks of life on the walls, an imperfect floor — no show-flat |
| P7 | Characters must hold a natural posture — a real stance (weight shifted to one side), an unforced expression (not a fully symmetrical smile), everyday hand movements |
| P8 | Must specify one concrete contemporary Chinese urban space — reject "generic city" or any invented city with no national markers |

### 4.2 Photographic-quality constraints (mandatory)

| No. | Rule |
|---|---|
| Q1 | Must state one "mode of presence" for the camera: fixed gaze (固定注视) / handheld breathing / gimbal flow / telephoto spying |
| Q2 | Depth of field must carry narrative intent — shallow depth for emotion, deep depth to stress the environment, rack focus to steer attention |
| Q3 | Highlights must not blow out to dead white and shadows must not go dead black without gradation — keeping highlight rolloff and shadow detail is the basis of a cinematic look |
| Q4 | Must state a preference for the grain texture of the image: 35mm film grain (fine and irregular) / clean sharp digital cinematography (default) |
| Q5 | Color mood must match the emotional scene — warm without color spill, cool without going muddy, neon without going cheap |

### 4.3 Strict prohibitions (highest priority, cannot be overridden)

| No. | Forbidden content |
|---|---|
| X1 | Strictly forbidden: "3D render / CG / UE / Blender / next-gen modelling / PBR materials / volumetric light / ambient occlusion (AO)" and every other CG term — this style speaks with light and camera, not with a render engine |
| X2 | Strictly forbidden: "2D hand drawing / illustration / animation / anime / manga / flat style" and every other non-photographic medium |
| X3 | Strictly forbidden: "ancient style / period costume / hanfu / xianxia / wuxia / Republican era / ancient architecture" and every other non-contemporary-Chinese-urban element |
| X4 | Strictly forbidden: "cyberpunk / sci-fi / interstellar / western fantasy / medieval / steampunk / other worlds" and every other non-contemporary worldview |
| X5 | Strictly forbidden: "fluorescent colors / neon color spill / hot Barbie pink / synthetic electronic colors / cheap high-saturation grading" |
| X6 | Strictly forbidden: "over-smoothed skin / plastic dummy look / pore-free wax face / AI-looking mask / symmetrical frozen smile" |
| X7 | Strictly forbidden: "deformed limbs / fused fingers / broken facial features / wrong human anatomy / uncanny valley" |
| X8 | Strictly forbidden: "low resolution / blur / noise explosion / heavy artifacts / white cut-out edges / stitching seams" |
| X9 | Strictly forbidden: "violence and gore / horror and thriller / nudity / sexual suggestion / vulgarity / borderline content / illegal content" |
| X10 | Strictly forbidden: "watermark / text / signature / logo / border / UI elements / traces of the generation tool" |

### 4.4 Judging a style breach

> When any of the following signals appears in the image you generate, you have already drifted off the Live-Action Urban style:

| Breach signal | Explanation |
|---|---|
| Skin looks like silicone | Over-smoothed, the live-action texture is gone — stop using words like "smooth" or "perfect skin" immediately |
| Light with no traceable source | The frame is bright but you cannot say where the light comes from — complete the light-source logic, reject "evenly lit everywhere" |
| No Chinese signage on the buildings | The city has lost its Chinese identity — add shop signs, street signs, bus stops and other unmistakable Chinese urban markers |
| The frame is too clean | The scene looks freshly renovated — add traces of life |
| The character stands like a model | Posture stiff and symmetrical — give the body a natural shift of weight |
| Colors look like a filter app | Grading unified but cheap — go back to driving color by light-source temperature, do not "apply a filter" |
