# Live-Action Urban Prop Image Generation · Constraint Manual

---

## 1. Prop design principles

> A live-action urban prop is not "a modelled object" but "an object that has been used" — it has sat in someone's hand, stood on some tabletop, been carried in some pocket. What the camera photographs is its present moment.

1. **An object is narrative** — every prop is a miniature vessel of story. The lipstick mark on a coffee cup implies someone was just drinking from it, the wear on a phone case implies long use, the creases in an umbrella imply it has been opened and closed again and again
2. **Traces of use before perfect condition** — a live-action urban prop must show evidence of "having been used". Brand-new, just-unboxed, sample-grade props never appear
3. **Real objects of contemporary Chinese city life** — props must be objects that genuinely exist in Chinese urban life: Alipay/WeChat Pay screens, domestic phone brands, Chinese-language packaging, Chinese-style takeaway containers — no stateless invented objects
4. **Material is reality** — not a material rendered from PBR parameters, but the real behaviour of a material photographed by a camera: the reflection of stainless steel, the glaze of ceramic, the light absorption of paper, the injection-moulding texture of plastic
5. **Single-view still-life photography** — the prop is presented on its own as a single still-life product photograph, not a multi-angle modelling display, not a 2×2 grid

## 3. Single-view still-life photography specification

> A live-action urban prop needs only a single still-life product photograph, not a multi-angle modelling display. Below is the complete specification for single-view photography.

### Frame composition

| Item | Photographic requirement |
|---|---|
| Prop presentation | The prop is displayed on its own, centred or on a third line, complete in frame, no cropping |
| Background | Plain grey seamless backdrop paper #B0B0B0 (matte), or a plain white tabletop/counter (light wood grain/off-white tabletop), chosen according to the nature of the prop. No extra objects, no ornament, no text |
| Light | Natural window light or single-source soft light. Window light from 45° at the side (bright and soft with natural shadow), or softbox from above + side fill (product-photography-grade even light). Light soft, direction clear, no hard shadows |
| Depth of field | Shallow depth of field (f/2.8-f/5.6), the prop itself sharp and crisp, slight softening front and back to isolate it |
| Cast shadow | The natural contact shadow between the base of the prop and the surface it rests on — real, not cut out and not floating. The shadow is soft and its direction matches the key light |
| Aspect ratio | 1:1 square or 4:3 recommended, suited to displaying a single prop |

### Prop state criteria

| Dimension | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Traces of use | There must be visual evidence of "having been used" — not brand new and unopened, not sample-grade condition |
| Chinese identity | Chinese-language marks/Chinese brands/Chinese-style packaging/contemporary Chinese everyday life — no stateless invented objects |
| Material realism | Not a rendered quality — the real behaviour of a material photographed by a camera (reflection, diffusion, transmission of light) |
| Sense of scale | The real size is implied by the density of the material texture and the craft detail; no reference object is needed |

## 4. Materials and surfaces — real objects under the camera

> What follows describes how the camera sees the surfaces of everyday urban objects; these are not render parameters.

| Material | Real behaviour under the camera | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Metal (stainless steel/aluminium alloy) | Brushed texture visible, edge highlights crisp but not blown out, reflecting the soft ambient colour around it, fingerprints and fine scratches on the surface | brushed metal texture, natural environmental reflection, fine scratches and fingerprints on the surface, sheen that is not rendered |
| Glass | Clear but with a faint colour of its own (a pale green at the cut edge), fingerprints or water marks on the surface, light refracting at the chamfered edge | glass naturally clear, real fingerprints/water marks on the surface, light refraction at the edge, not CG-perfect transparency |
| Ceramic | Soft reflection off the glaze (not mirror-like), fine crazing/scratches on the surface, wear at the rim and base | soft reflection off ceramic glaze, fine scratches from use, natural wear at the base |
| Plastic | Injection-moulding texture/parting line visible, fine scratches on the surface, the diffuse quality of matte plastic | injection-moulding texture clear, parting line naturally visible, fine scratches from use on the surface |
| Paper | Fibre roughness visible, creases natural (not deliberate), printed text slightly bled, edges worn from being handled | paper fibre texture, natural creases, real quality of printed text, edges naturally worn |
| Textile (cotton/linen/canvas) | Weave texture visible, fuzz/pilling on the surface, creases and wrinkles from use, slight unevenness of colour | textile weave texture, natural fuzz and pilling, real wrinkles after use |
| Leather | Natural grain/pores visible, marks of use where it bends, natural wear at the edges, oxidation on the hardware | natural leather grain, marks of use where it bends, slight oxidation on the hardware, real patina from use |
| Wood | Natural grain, scratches/knocks on the surface, wear on the finish, slight colour change | natural clear wood grain, marks of knocks from use on the surface, everyday wear on the finish |

## 6. Constraint rules

### Mandatory

| No. | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | Must be "a single still-life photograph" — single view, not multi-angle, not a 2×2 grid, not a modelling display |
| R2 | Must specify "plain grey backdrop paper #B0B0B0" or a real tabletop such as "light wood-grain tabletop/off-white counter"; complex scenes are forbidden |
| R3 | Must specify the light-source logic — window light or softbox, direction of light and shadow clear, cast shadow natural |
| R4 | The prop must carry "marks of use" — not brand new, not sample-grade condition, not unopened |
| R5 | The prop must be recognisable as belonging to a contemporary Chinese city — Chinese-language marks/Chinese brands/Chinese-style packaging |
| R6 | Must state the core anchor "real-object product photography + not 3D rendered not CG" |
| R7 | Materials must be described as they behave under the camera, not as render parameters |

### Strictly forbidden

| No. | Strictly forbidden |
|---|---|
| X1 | Strictly forbidden: "3D render / 3D modelling / CG / UE engine / Blender / PBR material" and every other CG term |
| X2 | Strictly forbidden: "2D hand-drawn / illustration / animation / anime" and other non-photographic media |
| X3 | Strictly forbidden: "multi-angle / four views / 2×2 grid / orthographic view / turnaround / blueprint" — a single image only |
| X4 | Strictly forbidden: props that are "brand new and unopened / sample-grade condition / with no marks of use / in perfect condition" |
| X5 | Strictly forbidden: "ancient style/antiquity/xianxia/wuxia/Republican era/cyberpunk/sci-fi/Western fantasy/invented worlds" and other non-contemporary-urban objects |
| X6 | Strictly forbidden: "invented objects with no Chinese identity / all-English packaging / non-Chinese brands" |
| X7 | Strictly forbidden: "any person/hand/finger/limb/prosthetic appearing" |
| X8 | Strictly forbidden: the prop being "held/gripped/worn/in use/interacting with a person" |
| X9 | Strictly forbidden: "floating/cut out on white/no cast shadow/no supporting surface" — the object must sit on a real surface |
| X10 | Strictly forbidden: "watermark / text / LOGO / signature / border / traces of AI generation" |
