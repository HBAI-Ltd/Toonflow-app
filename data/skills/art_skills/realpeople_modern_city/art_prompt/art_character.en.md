# Live-Action Urban Character Base Likeness Generation · Constraint Manual

---

## 1. Casting logic — the person in front of the camera

> A 3D character starts from "build a model"; live-action urban starts from "pick a person and put them in front of the camera". The principles below proceed from casting and shooting, not from modelling.

1. **The face is the narrative** — a face is a story that has not been written down. Live-action urban does not chase "perfect features", it chases "features worth looking at" — recognisable asymmetry, small flaws that carry a story, a face the camera can dwell on
2. **Ordinary-person quality over star quality** — urban narrative needs someone "who looks like the people you meet on the subway every day", not someone "who looks like they just stepped off a magazine cover". A real bare face takes priority over polished hair and makeup
3. **Posture tells the truth** — a real standing pose is not symmetrical: weight on one leg, shoulders slightly off level, neck naturally tipped forward or back. Never use model-style symmetrical stances or military attention posture
4. **The same person must be recognisable across angles** — the four angles front/side/back/close-up must read as a photographic record of one person, not four similar people. Core identification points: jawline contour, the break of the nose bridge, ear shape, hairline direction

## 4. Skin quality — skin under the camera

> Live-action urban skin quality is not a render parameter but the behaviour of light on real skin: reflection, transmission and scattering all depend on the skin's own oil, moisture, pigment and years.

### Female

| Dimension | Photographic requirement | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Skin tone base | The real skin-tone range of Asian urban women: cool fair to warm fair, with slight unevenness allowed (redder cheekbones, darker eye area) — that is the evidence of a real person | real Asian female skin tone, natural unevenness of skin tone, natural redness over the cheekbones, real texture around the eyes |
| Surface sheen | Neither matte nor greasy — the reflection of natural oil on the T-zone and the natural highlight on the cheekbone high point are signs that the skin is "alive" | natural skin sheen, real T-zone oil reflection, skin that is alive rather than modelled |
| Texture visibility | Shot at 50mm from one metre: pores faintly visible above the cheekbones, pores clear on either side of the nose wings, fine lines between the brows naturally present | visible real skin pores, real texture on the nose wings, natural fine lines between the brows |
| Flaws retained | Retained but not emphasised: scattered small moles, pale freckles, faint old acne marks, surface capillaries — these are the evidence of a "person", not bugs to be fixed | real small moles retained, natural pale freckles, the imperfect texture of real human skin |
| Strictly forbidden | Plastic skin smoothing, waxwork mask, zero-pore silicone feel, uniform matte across the whole face, AI-smooth skin | — |

### Male

| Dimension | Photographic requirement | Prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Skin tone base | The real skin-tone range of Asian urban men: cool fair to healthy wheat, with sun-exposure colour difference allowed (natural transition between face and neck, darker T-zone, darker eye area) | real Asian male skin tone, natural sun-exposure colour difference, real skin-tone transition between face and neck |
| Surface sheen | T-zone oil reflection (highlights on forehead/nose bridge), the faint sheen of a freshly shaved jaw — male skin sheen comes from oil, not from highlighter | natural T-zone oil sheen, real freshly shaved facial texture, real rather than retouched skin reflection |

## 7. Base outfit — the one that has actually been worn

> The live-action urban base outfit is not a "base layer" but "the piece hanging on the back of the chair, worn several times, that carries body memory".

### Female base outfit

A plain basic top (crew-neck T-shirt/cotton shirt/knit cardigan) + basic bottoms (straight-leg jeans/cotton trousers/knee-length skirt), in everyday urban basic colours — black/white/grey/beige/navy/khaki. The fabric shows natural texture (the soft creases of cotton, the twill of denim, the loop texture of knitwear). No brand marks, no large prints, no decorative design.

### Male base outfit

A plain basic top (crew-neck T-shirt/long-sleeve henley/cotton shirt) + basic bottoms (straight-leg jeans/cotton casual trousers), in everyday urban basic colours — black/white/grey/navy/khaki. The fabric shows natural texture (the slight crumple of washed cotton, the fading and wear of denim, the natural curl of a shirt collar). No brand marks, no large prints, no decorative design.

### Unified dressing rules

- The base outfit is not a "base layer" — it is a complete piece of everyday wear in itself, only stripped of every decorative element
- The clothing carries marks of "having been worn": hem naturally slightly creased, cuffs marked by being pulled on and off, slight stretch texture at the knees/elbows
- Coverage: essentially covered apart from face/neck/hands, at the coverage level of everyday urban dress, with no excessive exposure
- Garment cut, colour and material are exactly the same across the four angles, reading as one and the same garment photographed from different angles

## 8. Character portrait series — four-angle photography specification

> What follows is not a 3D-modelling "four-view turnaround" but four camera angles on the same person within **one and the same studio session**. Light, clothing and the subject's state must read as a continuous photographic record.

### View definitions

| Position | Angle | Shot size | Photographic requirement | Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Far left | Front, medium close-up | Top of head to top of collarbone | The face fills 60% or more of the frame. Focal length 50mm or 85mm, shallow depth of field (f/2.8-f/4), focus on the pupils. Light soft and even, with a natural catchlight in the eyes. Hairline to collarbone shown complete, crown not cropped | portrait closeup, head to collarbone, shallow depth of field, eyes in sharp focus, natural catchlight, live-action photography |
| Second from left | Front 0° | Full body | The subject faces the camera, arms hanging naturally or slightly bent at the sides. Focal length 35mm or 50mm. Moderate depth of field (f/5.6-f/8), whole body sharp, head to feet complete in frame | front view, full body, facing camera, head to toe complete, live-action full body portrait |
| Second from right | Right side 90° | Full body | The subject faces sideways to the camera, showing a pure profile. The side outlines of nose bridge, lips, jaw, shoulders and back, and legs are clearly readable. Hair reads naturally from the side. Head to feet complete in frame | profile view, full body, side angle 90 degrees, head to toe complete, natural side profile |
| Far right | Rear 180° | Full body | The subject faces away from the camera. Shows the hairstyle at the back of the head, the posture of the back, and the heels. The hairline at the nape, loose strands behind the ears, and the back of the collar are naturally visible. Head to feet complete in frame | back view, full body, rear angle 180 degrees, head to toe complete, live-action back portrait |

### Frame specification

| Item | Photographic requirement |
|---|---|
| Layout | Four angles side by side in one frame, left to right, evenly spaced, no overlap. Laid out as a "casting photo / character reference board" |
| Background | Mid-grey seamless backdrop paper, value around #B0B0B0, no seams, no gradient, no light spots, no cast shadow. The slight texture of the backdrop paper is visible, proving this was shot rather than rendered |
| Standing pose | The stance of someone "the photographer asked to stand up straight and look natural" — not attention, not a model pose, but the moment of standing normally and suddenly being told to hold still |
| Consistency | The same person, the same session, the same clothing, the same lighting conditions — the four angles are a continuous photographic record of one person. Identification anchors: jawline contour, ear shape, hairline direction, habitual stance |
| Facial expression | Neutral micro-expression — the natural state of a subject facing the camera, neither deliberately smiling nor deliberately severe. A faint awareness of "looking at the lens" in the eyes |
| Light | Standard studio soft-light setup: large softbox key light from the front + reflector fill from both sides. Light soft, direction clear, no hard shadows, no split-lit face. Lighting ratio about 1:2 to 1:3, keeping the modelling of the face |
| Aspect ratio | 4:1 or 16:4 wide format recommended |
