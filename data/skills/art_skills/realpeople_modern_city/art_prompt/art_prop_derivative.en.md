# Live-Action Urban Prop Derived State Generation · Constraint Manual

---

## 1. Derivation principles

> Live-action urban prop derivation is not "swapping materials" but "what the same object has become when the camera photographs it again at a different time". Wear is the diary time writes on an object; stains are the signature life leaves behind.

1. **Form anchoring** — the prop's core form and silhouette must stay identifiable in every state. A phone used for two years has scratches on the screen and knocks on the frame, but one look still tells you it is that same model
2. **State must be readable** — new or old, intact or broken, dry or wet — distinguishable at a glance. The differences between states come from the physical logic of real life
3. **In service of narrative** — the state of a prop is a prologue that was never filmed. A smashed phone implies a quarrel, a water-soaked letter implies a downpour, a yellowed photograph implies time sealed away
4. **Progression must be plausible** — damage and ageing follow the physical laws of real materials: paper yellows, then goes brittle, then tears; metal oxidises, then rusts; fabric fades, then wears thin, then rips
5. **Single-view still-life photography** — a derived state needs only one still-life photograph. Same camera position, same light, same background as the original prop sheet — only the state of the prop has changed

## 2. State types

### 2.1 Use-and-time states

| State | Description | Applicable props | Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brand new | Just unboxed/just bought, intact, sheen full, no marks of use at all | All props | brand-new unboxed state, intact, full sheen, no scratches no wear, factory condition |
| Everyday use | Used for a while — evenly slight wear, natural patina, sheen where it is touched (buttons, grip), slight knocks at the corners. The real state of an object after normal use by a city dweller | Phone/wallet/keys/cup/watch/backpack and other everyday carried items | normal marks of use, natural patina at the grip, slight knocks at the corners, sheen on the button area, the real state of everyday use |
| Heavy use | Used a long time — obvious wear, sheen dulled, dense scratches on the surface, thick patina where it is touched, knocks at many corners, but function unaffected | Old phone/old wallet/long-used tool/old cup | heavy marks of use, dense surface scratches, knocks at many corners, thick patina, still fully usable |
| Aged | A strong sense of period — faded, oxidised, thick patina, material aged, slight deformation possible | Old photographs/old books/heirlooms/old receipts | strong sense of period, natural fading and oxidation, thick patina, material aged, the imprint left by time |

### 2.2 Damage states

| State | Description | Applicable props | Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slightly damaged | Small cracks/small chips/surface scratches/local wear — light damage that does not affect recognition of the object | Ceramic cup/phone screen/glasses/wooden items | fine cracks, shallow surface scratches, slight chips at the corners, light local wear, the object itself intact |
| Broken | Obvious cracks/shattering/local fracture — the structure is damaged but the whole is still recognisable | Shattered phone screen/cracked cup/torn pages/broken pen | shattered screen, cracks running through, local fracture, structure damaged, but the form of the object still identifiable |
| Fragment | Only fragments/the core part left — large areas missing, only the identifying features that anchor it to the original object remain | Porcelain shards/half a torn photograph/broken glass | fragment state, only the core part left, large areas missing, shards, the outline of the original object still identifiable |

### 2.3 Environmental-trace states

| State | Description | Applicable props | Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rained on/soaked | Caught in the rain/dropped in water — beads of water/a film of water on the surface, paper soaked and wrinkled, ink slightly bled, water marks on metal surfaces | Phone/book/letter/wallet/umbrella | beads of water condensed on the surface, paper soaked and slightly wrinkled, ink lightly bled, water marks left on the metal surface, the real state of having just been rained on |
| Stained | Everyday stains — coffee, tea, food grease, ink, mud spots, dust, all consistent with the logic of city life | Cup/clothing/book/desktop items | coffee ring, tea stain soaked in, food grease, everyday mud spots and dust, real marks left by living |
| Dusty/dust-covered | Left untouched for a while — a thin layer of dust on the surface, dust gathered in the corners, sheen buried under the dust | A book on the shelf/a photo frame on the sill/a box in the store room | a thin layer of dust over the surface, dust naturally gathered at the corners, the object's sheen softened by the dust, marks of having been set aside |
| Sun-faded | Long exposure to sunlight — colour lightened, paper yellowed, plastic embrittled and discoloured, textiles faded | A book by the window/outdoor items/things on the balcony | faded by sun exposure, colour lightened unevenly, paper edges yellowed, plastic discoloured and embrittled |

### 2.4 Emotional-narrative states

| State | Description | Applicable props | Prompt |
|---|---|---|---|
| Read through/well-thumbed | Page edges fuzzed, fold marks on the cover, cracks along the spine, adhesive residue from sticky notes — genuinely read | Books/diaries/photo albums/notebooks | page edges worn and fuzzed, natural fold marks on the cover, use cracks along the spine, real marks of being read a thousand times |
| Treasured/kept safe | Wrapped in cloth/a box/an envelope, the wrapping showing the marks of years but carefully handled | Keepsakes/photographs/jewellery/diaries | carefully wrapped and kept, the wrapping paper showing slight marks of the years, opened and carefully wrapped again, evidence of being treasured |
| Abandoned/forgotten | The state of being alone — covered in dust, pressed into a corner, the quality of being ignored | Old phone/old toy/old photograph/old letters | the abandoned feel of being covered in dust, marks of being pressed under clutter, the solitary state of being ignored |
| Held/loved and carried | The particular sheen polished into the touched areas by repeated contact — the corner of a wallet most often touched, around a phone's buttons, the grip of a pen | Wallet/phone/fountain pen/keyring/ring | a particular patina polished into the touched areas by repeated contact, the sheen of something loved and carried close, marks left by someone's body warmth |

## 3. Single-view photography specification

> Live-action urban prop derivation needs only a **single** still-life photograph. Same camera position, same light, same background — only the state of the prop has changed. Not a four-panel grid, not multi-angle, not a turnaround.

### This specification is shared by every state variant

| Item | Photographic requirement |
|---|---|
| Background | Plain grey seamless backdrop paper #B0B0B0 (matte), or a light wood-grain tabletop/off-white counter — **exactly the same** as the original prop sheet |
| Light | **Exactly the same** window light or softbox setup as the original prop sheet. Light position, lighting ratio and colour temperature unchanged |
| Angle | **Exactly the same** camera position and angle as the original prop sheet. Make sure it reads as "the same object photographed in the same position at a different time" |
| Depth of field | Shallow depth of field f/2.8-f/5.6, consistent with the original image |
| Composition | The prop displayed alone and centred, occupying 70%+ of the frame as the main subject, complete in frame with no cropping |
| Cast shadow | The natural contact shadow between the base of the prop and the surface it rests on, its direction matching the key light |
| Aspect ratio | 1:1 square or 4:3, consistent with the original image |

## 4. Changes of material state — the marks time leaves on real objects

> What follows describes how real materials age naturally in everyday city life; these are not render parameters.

| Material | Brand new → everyday use | Everyday → heavy use | How damage shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| Metal (stainless steel/aluminium alloy) | Mirror/brushed sheen full → fine scratches on the surface, slight patina in the fingerprint area, slight knocks at the corners | Dense scratches, sheen turned inward, local oxidation and discolouration, edges worn round | Dents, deformation, fracture, spots of rust |
| Glass | Clear and unscratched, edges smooth → shallow surface scratches, fingerprints left, slight chips at the edges | Scratches everywhere, clarity reduced, edges worn rough | Shattering, cracks, chipped corners, missing pieces |
| Ceramic | Glaze full and bright → fine surface scratches, wear at the rim and base, glaze softened | Glaze dulled, dense scratches, base worn through to the body, possible fine crazing | Cracks, chips, shattering, glaze flaking off |
| Plastic | Surface smooth, colour even → slight abrasion marks, sheen dropping, wear at the corners | Dense scratches, yellowing with age, sheen from oil where it is touched, slight deformation | Fracture, chips, broken edges, badly gouged surface |
| Paper | Brand new and flat, printing crisp → edges slightly fuzzed, natural slight wrinkling, marks of being handled | Yellowed and brittle, dense fold marks, edges worn and fuzzed, possible local damage | Tearing, missing corners, soaked and wrinkled, scorching, text blurred |
| Leather | Grain crisp, sheen even → patina from use, grain deepened where it bends, slight oxidation on the hardware | Thick patina, risk of cracking where it bends, colour dulled, edges worn | Cracking, fracture, surface flaking off, hardware rusted |
| Textile (cotton/linen/canvas) | Fibres neat, colour bright → slight fuzzing, colour slightly faded, normal wrinkles from use | Obvious fuzzing and pilling, obvious fading, thinning wear at the stress areas of elbows/knees/seat | Tearing, holes, worn through, seams coming apart |
| Wood | Grain crisp, finish smooth → fine scratches, warm patina, slight local wear of the finish | Dense scratches, finish flaking, wood colour dulled, corners worn round | Splitting, fracture, chips, marks of woodworm |
| Electronic screen/panel | Screen bright and clear, touch responsive, no scratches → fine scratches, oleophobic coating worn, slight burn-in | Dense scratches, local ageing and colour shift on the screen, frame worn | Shattering, leaking display, abnormal display, glitched image, unresponsive touch |

## 6. Constraint rules

### Mandatory

| No. | Rule |
|---|---|
| R1 | The prop's core form/silhouette must stay identifiable in every state; a change of state must never make it unrecognisable |
| R2 | The change of state must follow the physical logic of real materials — paper yellows before it goes brittle, metal oxidises before it rusts, with no jarring jumps |
| R3 | Must be a **single** still-life photograph — single view, not a 2×2 four-panel grid, not multi-angle, not a turnaround |
| R4 | Must keep the same camera position, the same light and the same background as the original prop sheet — only the state of the prop differs |
| R5 | Must specify state marks that are neither brand new nor sample-grade, unless the state itself is "brand new" |
| R6 | The degree of damage/ageing should be narratively justified — do not distress for the sake of distressing; the marks must imply "something happened" |
| R7 | Must state the core anchor "real-object product photography + not 3D rendered not CG" |

### Strictly forbidden

| No. | Strictly forbidden |
|---|---|
| X1 | Strictly forbidden: the prop becoming unrecognisable after the change of state; strictly forbidden: altering the prop's core form on your own initiative |
| X2 | Strictly forbidden: changes of state that break physical logic (ceramic rusting, plastic developing patina, glass corroding and other anti-physical behaviour) |
| X3 | Strictly forbidden: "3D render / 3D modelling / CG / PBR material / UE engine / Blender" and every other CG term |
| X4 | Strictly forbidden: "2D hand-drawn / illustration / animation / anime" and other non-photographic media |
| X5 | Strictly forbidden: "multi-angle / four views / 2×2 grid / orthographic view / turnaround / blueprint" — a single image only |
| X6 | Strictly forbidden: "ancient style/antiquity/xianxia/wuxia/Republican era/cyberpunk/sci-fi/Western fantasy" and other non-contemporary-urban objects |
| X7 | Strictly forbidden: "glowing/activating/energy light effects/magic/superpowers/sci-fi effects" — real objects do not glow |
| X8 | Strictly forbidden: "any person/hand/finger/limb appearing" |
| X9 | Strictly forbidden: the prop being "held/gripped/worn/in use" |
| X10 | Strictly forbidden: "floating/cut out on white/no cast shadow/no supporting surface" |
| X11 | Strictly forbidden: "excessively gory/horrific/disturbing depictions of damage" |
| X12 | Strictly forbidden: "watermark / text / LOGO / signature / border / traces of AI generation" |
