# Storyboard prompts · Live-Action Urban · Style-specific techniques

---

## Scope

This Skill is dedicated to generating storyboard prompts in the **Live-Action Urban** style, fitted to storyboard work for live-action film/TV subjects such as urban drama, workplace growth, youthful healing, street life and urban suspense.

---

## 1. Emotion in front of the camera — faces, gazes, micro-expressions

> The emotion of Live-Action Urban does not come from "cybernetic light effects" or "render parameters", but from the micro-expressions of a real actor in front of the camera. The mappings below are aimed at how a camera records real people.

| Emotion input | Face description | Gaze description | Micro-expression addition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Capable / composed | Crisp expression, facial lines natural and untensed | Certain gaze, looking straight ahead without wavering | Lip line straight, chin slightly tucked, presence steady and restrained |
| Sorrowful / desolate | Brows and eyes lowered, facial muscles slack | Gaze scattered and unfocused, toward the window or the floor | Corners of the mouth sinking naturally, a wistful look, unforced |
| Gentle / healing | Brows and eyes relaxed, face soft | Gaze warm and attentive, as if truly listening to the other person | Corners of the mouth lifting slightly — not a smile, but warmth |
| Cold / guarded | Facial lines slightly tightened, not exaggerated | Gaze sharp but not excessive, looking with force | Jaw tightened, expression restrained, body language defensive |
| Surprised / moved | Outer eye corners lifting slightly, brows rising naturally | Gaze snapping into focus, pupils slightly dilated | Mouth slightly open, expression alive but not exaggerated |
| Contemplative / introspective | Calm expression, muscles around the eyes relaxed | Gaze reaching far and unfocused, looking without seeing | Expression quiet, perhaps a hand under the chin or a finger tapping lightly |
| Joyful / vivid | Natural laugh lines at the eye corners, eyes curving slightly | Eyes bright and lit, laughter tugging at the corners | Corners of the mouth lifting, cheeks slightly flushed, expression lively but not exaggerated |
| Exhausted / weary | Eye sockets slightly sunken, the whole face slack | Gaze hazy, eyelids sinking slightly | Brows and eyes lowered, possible dark circles, expression loose |
| Expectant / longing | Brows and eyes lifting slightly, small restrained movements on the face | Eyes shining, light in the pupils | Corners of the mouth lifting faintly, a suppressed smile on the face |
| Resolute / determined | Facial lines clear, chin lifted slightly | Gaze straight ahead, neither evading nor dodging | Chin tucked or raised slightly, expression decisive but not fierce |

## 2. Light in front of the camera — light-source logic and atmosphere

### 2.1 Natural-light moments

| Moment | Light behavior | Color temperature and tone | Spatial atmosphere |
|---|---|---|---|
| Early morning | Low-angle warm white daylight entering the interior at an angle through the window; outdoors the sun has just cleared the buildings, long shadows | 3500-4500K warm white, air slightly cold | Quiet and not yet awake, morning mist crisp, nothing has begun |
| Morning | Daylight risen, cool white and clear, sharp shadows; outdoor building-surface materials clear; window-light area at its largest indoors | 5000-6000K neutral leaning cool | Order established, the day running, efficient and clear |
| Noon | Toplight or nearly toplight, shadows short and heavy; strong material reflections outdoors; window light entering from above indoors | 5500-6500K cool | A sense of pause, the midday gap, the peak of day after day |
| Afternoon | Daylight moving west, warm tone strengthening, angled light through a window or trees making long shadows and light patches | 4000-5000K warming | Languid, time slowing, the longest stretch of the day |
| Golden hour | Extremely low-angle warm gold diffusion, shadows at their longest, every surface gilded at the edge | 2800-3500K very warm | Fleeting preciousness, the only light for warm and memory passages |
| Blue hour | After the sun sinks below the horizon, deep blue-violet sky, natural light very dim and very cool, artificial sources just coming on | 8000-10000K sky + warm points of artificial light | Transition, poetry, day is over and night has not taken over |
| Late night | Only artificial sources — streetlights, desk lamps, screen light, headlights. Most of the frame sinks into shadow | Warm sources 2800-3200K / cool sources 4000-6500K | Loneliness or privacy, the world has gone quiet |

### 2.2 Emotional lighting

| Emotional keynote | Light recipe | Photographic constraint |
|---|---|---|
| Workplace restraint | Cool white window light as key (5000-5500K), possibly with cool screen light as facial fill, shadows keeping detail | Moderate lighting ratio about 1:3, keep spatial depth, glass partitions producing layered reflections |
| Everyday slack | Broad diffused window light (4500-5000K), softened by curtains, no hard shadows | Low-contrast clarity, shallow depth of field isolating the character |
| Warm home | Warm desk/floor lamp side light (2800-3200K), window light secondary (if daytime), shadows soft | Warm envelope, a single warm source dominant, shadows warm and detailed |
| Urban street life | Mixed sources — warm streetlight (2800K) + cool white shopfronts (4000K) + headlights sweeping past | Warm-dominant, cool light as local counterpoint, the immediacy of handheld shooting |
| Solitude on a rainy night | Wet ground reflecting streetlights (cool 6000K ambient base + warm 2800K spots), rain streaks on the window scattering light | Warm and cool coexisting, a single warm source on the face, everything else sinking into cool darkness |
| Suspense and tension | Hard light from the side, deep shadows, lighting ratio pushed far apart, warm-cool counterpoint (such as cool blue outside the window + warm yellow indoors) | High ratio 1:8 or more, hard light drawing the outline, shadows deep but with detail |
| Youthful healing | Ample natural light (5000-5500K), broad diffusion, skylight + ground reflection as fill | Low-contrast high key, clear and fresh, shallow depth of field softening the background |
| Late-night fragility | A single warm source (desk lamp 2800-3200K or a streetlight outside) lighting like an island, one side of the face lit and the other dark | Minimal sources, shadows rich in detail, ratio may reach 1:8 |

## 3. Scenes in front of the camera — the live-action texture of real places

| Scene type | Elements the camera must capture |
|---|---|
| Office tower/office | Layered reflection and transmission through glass partitions, striped light from blinds, personal items scattered on a desk, a half-finished bottle of water open on the meeting table, cool screen light on a face |
| Rented room/apartment | A clear direction of natural light from a floor-to-ceiling or small window, sticker residue and nail holes on the wall, a blanket thrown over the sofa, remote control and half a glass of water on the coffee table, slightly worn flooring |
| Café/milk tea shop | Warm light at the counter and steam from the coffee machine, the light at the window seat as the most precious, use marks on cups and trays, fingerprints on the floor-to-ceiling glass, the street outside as another layer |
| Old town/alleys | Mottled walls and creeping moss, the real urban grain of utility poles and overhead wires, warm yellow light from old streetlamps, laundry hung out to dry, street vendors and hand-written signs |
| City street/intersection | Crosswalk and traffic lights, street trees and lamps, road markings and their wear, shop windows and neon signs, a bus-stop sign and the gaps between people waiting |
| Convenience store (late night) | Cool white island lighting from fluorescent tubes, the orderliness of the shelves, the cool light and frosted glass of the freezer, the deep black street outside the window, the small warm corner of the staff area |
| Subway station/car | Cool white fluorescent lighting, glowing ad lightboxes, reflections on the platform screen doors, worn car seats, light flowing past in the tunnel |
| Rooftop | The full sweep of the city skyline, railings and ventilation ducts, the worn waterproof layer underfoot, clotheslines and clutter, the color temperature of the sky and the state of the clouds |
| Hospital corridor/ward | Cool fluorescent light in a long corridor, reflections on the floor, the clean and cold feel of white walls and handrails, the natural light outside the window as the only warm color |
| Campus/classroom | The arrangement and use marks of desks and chairs, chalk residue on the blackboard, natural light through the window into the classroom, the deep perspective of a corridor |

## 4. Camera grammar — lens choice and movement

### 4.1 Focal-length choice

| Focal length | Narrative character | Suitable scene |
|---|---|---|
| 24-28mm wide | The environment surrounds the character, spatial alienation, the vastness of the city against the smallness of the individual | Urban insert shots, looking down from a rooftop, alone in a crowd |
| 35mm mild wide | Subjective viewpoint, a documentary feel of being there, person and environment coexisting | Walking the streets, indoor dialogue, everyday following |
| 50mm standard | Equivalent to the human eye, an objective and neutral gaze, the most honest lens | Character portraits, two-person dialogue, emotional gazing |
| 85mm medium telephoto | Compresses space, shortens emotional distance, background falls off to isolate the character | Close-ups (特写), seen from far across the street, a voyeuristic viewpoint |
| 135mm+ telephoto | Peels the character off the environment, an extreme gaze or spying | The isolated individual in the city, gazing into the distance, narrative distance |

### 4.2 Shot-size choice

| Shot size | Framing range | Narrative function |
|---|---|---|
| 大特写 (extreme close-up) | Eyes/lips/fingers/detail of an object | Emotional flashpoint, revealing key information, sensory focus |
| 近景 (medium close-up) | Top of the head to above the chest | Character emotion, dialogue reaction, facial micro-expression |
| 中近景 (medium-close shot) | Top of the head to the waist | Character interaction, gestures, body language in dialogue |
| 中景 (medium shot) | Top of the head to around the knees | The first-level relation between character and space, several people in one frame |
| 全景 (wide shot) | The character's full body + part of the environment | The character's position in the space, the path of movement |
| 大全景/空镜 (wide establishing shot/insert shot) | The whole space, the character tiny or absent | Spatial narrative, emotional buffer, metaphorical pause |

### 4.3 Camera movement

| Movement | Visual psychology | Use case |
|---|---|---|
| 固定 (locked off) | Calm observation, letting time speak for itself | Everyday scenes, dialogue, insert shots as breathing space |
| 手持微晃 (slight handheld sway) | Presence, breathing, subjective involvement | Emotional swings, street walking, intimate following |
| 稳定器流动 (gimbal flow) | An elegant urban stroll, poetry | Major scene transitions, a character's entrance, showing a space |
| 缓推 (slow push in) | Attention gradually focusing, emotion drawing near | Dialogue heating up, truth closing in, emotion moving forward |
| 缓拉 (slow pull back) | Opening the distance, revealing the whole picture, emotional detachment | Parting, closing a chapter, showing a space |
| 跟拍 (following shot) | Walking with the character, subjective company | Walking the city, tracking, everyday following |
| 摇镜 (pan) | Shifting the line of sight, laying out spatial relations | Guiding the character's line of sight, the relation between one space and another |

## 5. Fixed style anchor words

**Photographic anchoring (mandatory):**

live-action photography, still from a live-action film, contemporary Chinese city, cinema-grade cinematography, staging of natural and artificial light, real color science

**Character texture (mandatory when the shot contains a character):**

live-action photographic realism, real skin texture with visible pores, no retouching and no silicone face, natural hair texture with loose strands, visible fabric texture with wearing creases, natural micro-expression, real body language

**Scene texture (mandatory when the shot contains a scene):**

live-action urban scene photography, real building materials and marks of age (mottled walls/worn floors/fingerprints on glass), light direction clear and traceable, natural aerial perspective, not a show flat and not an architectural rendering

**Reference-image consistency anchoring (mandatory in reference-image mode):**

keep the character's look, face, hairstyle and clothing exactly consistent with the reference image, keep the scene style consistent with the reference image, keep the lighting and color keynote unified, present all shots as a continuous photographic record of the same person

**Style closing (fixed):**

live-action photographic realism, contemporary Chinese urban film texture, cinema-grade photographic image, not 3D, not rendered, not CG

**Image-quality lock words:**

Mode A (English) — default:
live-action photography, high-definition image quality, cinema-grade image texture, no subtitles, no watermark, no title overlay and no UI elements in the frame

Mode A (English) — in-frame text scenes (when the frame contains prop text such as signboards/street signs/screens/books):
live-action photography, high-definition image quality, cinema-grade image texture, no subtitles, no watermark, no title overlay and no UI elements in the frame, text on scene props such as signboards/street signs/screens/books clearly legible

Mode B (English) — default:
live-action cinematic photography, real human skin texture, contemporary urban China, cinematic color science, natural light photography, high detail, no subtitles, no captions, no watermark, no title overlay, no UI elements

Mode B (English) — in-frame text scenes:
live-action cinematic photography, real human skin texture, contemporary urban China, cinematic color science, natural light photography, high detail, no subtitles, no captions, no watermark, no title overlay, no UI elements, legible text on in-scene props such as signboards, street signs, screens and books

**Negative-word template (mode B must contain it, placed at the end of the prompt):**

> ⚠️ Seedream (mode A) does not support negative prompts; negative words apply to mode B only. Mode A guarantees image quality through the texture anchoring and image-quality lock words in the positive prompt.

Mode B (English):
no 3D render, no CGI, no Unreal Engine, no Blender, no PBR material, no game engine, no 2D cartoon, no anime, no illustration, no hand drawn, no painting, no plastic skin, no wax face, no airbrushed, no poreless skin, no silicone face, no ancient Chinese style, no traditional costume, no cyberpunk, no sci-fi, no western fantasy, no medieval, no non-Chinese urban, no plastic mannequin, no symmetrical model pose, no branded new clothes, no showroom, no wrong anatomy, no deformed face, no broken limbs, no distorted body, no subtitles, no captions, no watermark, no title overlay, no UI text

---

## 6. Aesthetic prohibitions

The following words/styles must not appear in the output prompt:

- ❌ 3D render / 3D modelling / CG / UE engine / Blender / PBR material / next-gen modelling / volumetric light / ambient occlusion / ray tracing
- ❌ 2D hand drawing / illustration / animation / anime / manga / chibi / low poly
- ❌ Photorealistic rendering (in English, photorealistic / realistic photography are forbidden — Live-Action Urban is "live-action cinematic photography", not "simulated rendering")
- ❌ Over-smoothed skin / plastic mask face / zero pores / silicone face / waxwork look / AI-smooth
- ❌ Ancient style / period costume / hanfu / xianxia / wuxia / Republican era / cyberpunk / sci-fi / western fantasy / medieval
- ❌ Wrong human anatomy / deformed limbs / broken facial features / wrong proportions
- ❌ Highly saturated fluorescent colors / neon color spill / cheap filter look
- ❌ Invented scenes that are not a contemporary Chinese city / a generic city with no Chinese signage

## 8. Quick reference card

### Emotion → image-word lookup

| Emotion | Face keywords | Matching light |
|---|---|---|
| Capable/composed | Crisp expression, certain gaze | Cool white window light as key + natural contrast on the outline |
| Sorrowful/desolate | Brows and eyes lowered, gaze scattered | Cool diffused light + low contrast, a single source from the side |
| Gentle/healing | Brows and eyes relaxed, gaze warm | Broad soft diffusion + warm window light |
| Cold/guarded | Face slightly tense, gaze sharp | Hard light from the side + high ratio with warm-cool counterpoint |
| Joyful/vivid | Laugh lines at the eye corners, bright gaze | Ample natural light + high key and clear |
| Contemplative/introspective | Calm expression, gaze reaching far | A single warm source + weak ambient light |
| Exhausted/weary | Eye sockets slightly sunken, gaze hazy | Soft diffused light + low contrast and low saturation |
| Determined/resolute | Chin lifted slightly, gaze straight | Hard light from the side + clear rim light |
