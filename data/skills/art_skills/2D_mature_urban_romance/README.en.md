# Mature Urban Romance Anime Style

This style is built specifically for the **mature urban romance anime** theme. All art prompts, specifications, and generated content are strictly limited to:

- **Anime style**: All characters, scenes, and props follow anime as the standard, emphasizing cel shading, clean lines, and dramatic low-key lighting.
- **Modern urban romance setting**: Modern urban life scenes, covering apartments, offices, cafes, city streets, etc., with no Guofeng (classical Chinese) or fantasy elements.
- **Cinema-level composition**: Composition emphasizes a cinematic feel — depth-of-field blur, lens optical characteristics, and aerial perspective.
- **Low-saturation cool tones**: Overall color leans cool, with warm tones used only as accents, highlighting a mood that is both cool-detached and tender at once.

## Scope of Use

- AI art generation for mature urban romance themes (characters, scenes, props, storyboards, etc.)
- Visual work adapting modern novels into an anime style
- Visual work requiring clean lines, cel shading, and cinema-level composition

## Strictly Forbidden

- Live-action / photographic / 3D-rendered realism
- Highly saturated fluorescent colors / neon colors
- Any style other than modern elements, such as Guofeng / fantasy / sci-fi
- Suggestive, exposed, or see-through content or other unsuitable material

## Style Experience

Under this style, you will experience:

- Characters with delicate faces and a cool or gentle temperament; clothing, makeup, and hairstyles all in the modern urban anime style
- Scenes with rich spatial layering, ultra-crisp material texture, and an atmosphere that is authentic and poetic
- Props, decor, and accessories all in modern urban form, finely crafted with an exquisite texture
- Storyboards and video both built on anime style, emphasizing narrative and emotional expression

---

This style applies only to "mature urban romance anime" related projects. For other styles (such as live-action realism, Guofeng, or fantasy), please do not use this prompt set.
