# Urban Romance Constraints · Mature Urban Romance Anime · Technique Reference

---

## 1. Tonal system and the visual keynote

- **Tonal base** — the whole piece takes cool-white skin (C1), light blue (C3) and blue-gray (C6) as its base colors; overall color temperature leans cool (5800-7000K) and saturation is medium-low (30-50%), giving a cool, restrained, refined gray tone
- **Warm-cool narrative contrast** — warm colors (amber warm C7, hazy rose C5) act as the visual signal of a narrative turn, used as local accents in passages of emotional re-warming and warming ambiguity. Warm-cool switches should be in step with the story arc, not mixed at random
- **Warm-cool allocation strategy** — separation/oppression/coldness passages strictly keep the cool base (blue-gray C6 + light blue C3) and must not use amber warm, hazy rose or other warm tones; warm color is released only after the emotion re-warms, using the warm-cool contrast to mark the narrative turn
- **Palette-first principle** — passage planning must bind an emotional scene first (first-meeting flutter/warming ambiguity/protective promise/separation and misunderstanding/reunion and release/night banquet lantern fair, etc.), then settle the main + secondary colors and the lighting scheme, so you never get "the plot is right but the emotion is the wrong color"
- **Forbidden color range** — highly saturated fluorescent colors, neon colors and modern digital color systems are all incompatible with this style

## 2. Lighting scheme system

- **Light is narrative** — 7 lighting schemes correspond to different emotional passages; at the director's planning stage the lighting keynote direction should be settled at passage level, not specified shot by shot
- **Cel-shading light characteristics** — a clear light-dark boundary and dramatic low-key lighting are the core means; light must serve the emotional narrative and keep the cel-shaded anime style

| Lighting scheme | Scheme name | Tonal leaning | Suitable emotion |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | Soft side-backlight | Cool white base + local warm rim light | Heart-flutter, tender devotion, everyday warmth |
| B | Hard side high contrast | Ink black C4 + silver gray C8 hard-light division | Confrontation and pressure, cold anger, tense opposition |
| C | Diffuse cool light | Blue-gray C6 + light blue C3 low-key lighting | Oppression and sorrow, separation and misunderstanding, lonely desolation |
| D | Scattered warm light | Amber warm C7 + hazy rose C5 locally | Warming ambiguity, reunion and release, intimate sweetness |
| E | Natural window light | Plain white C10 + light blue C3 dappled side light | Interior daytime, everyday living, workplace commuting |
| F | Night lamplight | Amber warm C7 accents + blue-gray C6 shadow areas | Night banquet lantern fair, city night view, romantic flowing light |
| G | Cool blue moonlight | Light blue C3 + silver gray C8 cool light | Night solitude, longing in solitude, cold detachment |

- **Warm-cool light allocation** — warm light (amber warm C7) suits passages of emotional re-warming and intimate ambiguity; cool light (blue-gray C6 + light blue C3) suits passages of separation, oppression and coldness. The director may adjust the warm-cool switch point as the narrative requires
- **Atmosphere direction mapping** — the atmosphere direction of every scene should map onto one of the lighting schemes above (A-G), ensuring visual consistency

## 3. Texture direction

- **Cel shading as the anchor** — the core of Mature Urban Romance Anime: clean lines, cel shading, dramatic low-key lighting, producing a cinema-level anime image
- **Face and skin** — finely rendered features, delicate skin, refined features; female cool-white skin (C1) is translucent not deathly pale, male warm-white skin (C2) is fresh and natural. The skin keeps the smooth, fine cel-shaded feel
- **Clothing material** — clear fabric texture on the garment, ultra-crisp texture detail, real fabric creases, clothing flowing with the movement. The fabric feel of modern urban clothing (suit/shirt/one-piece dress/coat) must be believable
- **Scene material** — clear scene material texture, aerial perspective, modern marks of use, a lived-in feel. Glass reflections, metallic sheen, wood grain and the drape of fabric must all read clearly within the cel-shaded style
- **Cel shading is not flatness** — Mature Urban Romance Anime emphasises layering cinema-level lighting and depth-of-field blur on top of the cel shading, amplifying the impact through composition and emotional design rather than relying on piled-up realism

## 4. Urban scene space elements

Scene elements peculiar to the modern urban world and their visual narrative functions:

- **Floor-to-ceiling windows/glass partitions/door frames** — natural framing props for composition, creating a "cannot see through" layering and spatial depth. The reflection and transmission of glass are the highlights of the frame
- **City skyline/streets/parks** — natural carriers of negative-space composition, where the view is the feeling: a bustling street scene = clamour within loneliness, sitting alone on a bench in the rain = solitude, fallen leaves drifting across the street = the sorrow of parting
- **Lamplight/window light/screen light** — the light sources of the modern city: warm indoor lamps = warmth/intimacy (scheme D), city night view = warm and cool interwoven (scheme F), natural window light = everyday/quiet (scheme E)
- **Use empty scene shots to transition between passages** — this style has rich scene assets (variants by time of day/weather), so it is recommended to use an empty scene shot as an emotional buffer between passages instead of a hard cut. The city skyline, a rainy view out of the window and an empty cafe can all serve as transition shots
- **Use visuals rather than dialogue at turning points** — prefer visual means (a sudden shift in lighting, a shot-size jump cut, an empty-shot metaphor) over relying on dialogue to explain

## 5. Urban score and ambient sound

Sound-element constraints under the modern urban romance world:

### Instrument choice for the score

- **Piano** — the core instrument, with the widest range of use: solo for solitude/longing/memory passages, light touch for everyday/heart-flutter passages
- **Strings (violin/cello/string section)** — the emotional bed and its elevation; the cello's low register suits oppression/restraint, the violin's high register suits heart-flutter, the string section suits the emotional climax
- **Guitar (acoustic/electric)** — acoustic guitar for everyday warmth/relaxed leisure passages, clean-tone electric guitar for city night views/a tipsy mood
- **Electronic atmosphere** — a low-frequency electronic pad beds the atmosphere for city empty shots/solitude passages, adding the spatial feel of the modern city
- **Flute/clarinet** — accents in gentle passages, never leading, used to support the mood of early morning/parks/everyday life

### Score combination strategy

| Emotional stage | Instrument combination |
|---|---|
| Steady/opening/closing | Piano solo or piano + light strings |
| Heart-flutter | Piano + violin |
| Warming ambiguity/intimacy | Acoustic guitar + light piano + string bed |
| Oppression/sorrow/separation | Cello solo or cello + piano |
| Emotional outburst/reunion/climax | Full string section + piano |
| City night view/solitude | Electronic atmosphere + piano |
| Everyday warmth/leisure | Acoustic guitar + flute + light percussion |

### Urban ambient sound

- **Typical ambient sound layers** — distant traffic / rain on the window glass / a coffee machine running / keyboard typing / high heels on the floor / an elevator chime / city night wind / a car horn in the distance / birdsong in the park / the low hum of a restaurant
- **Mark 1-2 core ambient sounds for each scene**, to help the later sound design. The richer the ambient sound layers, the more immersive the urban scene
