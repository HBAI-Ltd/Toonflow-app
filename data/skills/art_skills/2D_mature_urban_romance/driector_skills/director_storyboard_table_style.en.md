# Storyboard-table Urban Romance Constraints · Mature Urban Romance Anime · Technique Reference

---

## 1. What the storyboard table is for

The storyboard table is the director's core tool for turning the script into camera language. What follows is only the constraint specification of the Mature Urban Romance Anime style at the storyboard-table level.

---

## 2. Light and atmosphere

- **One lighting scheme per scene** — no more than two lighting schemes should appear within one scene, unless there is a clear narrative turn (interior lights switching → dusk deepening outside the window, clear sky → sudden drizzle)
- **Emotion-palette binding** — every scene binds at least 1 emotional scene (first-meeting flutter/warming ambiguity/protective promise/separation and misunderstanding/reunion and release/night banquet lantern fair) and keeps the main + secondary color continuous within the shot group
- **Light-angle specification** — shots facing the character default to soft side-backlight or diffuse light; shots from the side adjust as the frame requires. When describing light, distinguish natural light (window light/sunlight) from artificial light (lamplight/screen light)
- **Match warm-cool tones to the narrative stage** — separation/oppression/coldness passages strictly use cool light (blue-gray C6 + light blue C3) and must not use amber warm/hazy rose or other warm tones; warm tones (amber warm C7 + hazy rose C5) are used only in passages of emotional re-warming and warming ambiguity
- **A lighting transition is an advanced device** — natural light outside the window fading into warm indoor lamplight = time passing from day to night; a bright office cutting to a dim apartment = an emotional turn. Mark the lighting change points in the storyboard table

---

## 3. Environmental motion

- **Environmental motion gives the frame room to breathe** — curtains stirring, steam rising from a coffee cup, raindrops sliding down the window glass, city lights flickering, leaves drifting down. Arrange at least one shot with environmental motion every 3-4 shots, so the frame does not go "dead"
- **Modern urban environmental elements first** — environmental motion should use elements from within the modern urban world: curtains stirring, light from passing traffic, rain threads across the glass, fallen leaves drifting across the street, coffee steam rising, the halo of a street lamp flickering; Guofeng/fantasy elements must not appear
- **Cel-shaded motion expression** — environmental motion should follow anime's way of showing it: the hand-drawn feel of dappled light, the linear beauty of fabric moving, the cel-shaded rendering of rain threads and lamplight

---

## 4. Action rhythm for urban characters

- **Action must be restrained** — mature urban romance characters should move in a reserved, understated way, with emotion held in. Mark key actions (turning around, raising a hand, looking back) as "slow" or "unhurried"
- **Clothing motion** — the small motions of modern urban clothing (suit hems, skirt hems, scarves, coat tails) are a natural asset for the frame; you may add the slight movement of clothing in the wind or with the action to the shot description, making the frame feel "alive"
- **Bearing constraints** — an urban romance character's bearing should match a mature, elite temperament: upright and unhurried stance, elegant and restrained gestures, and no exaggerated anime-style body language (large gestures, chibi-deformed expressions)
- **Micro-expressions first** — convey emotion through facial micro-expressions (the curve of the mouth, a slight lift of the brow, a shift of the gaze) rather than relying on large body movements

---

## 5. Camera-movement taboos

- **Fast camera movement is banned** — whip pans (甩镜), sudden push-ins and handheld shake clash with the cool, elegant temperament of Mature Urban Romance Anime
- **Fancy transitions are banned** — wipes, spins, venetian blinds, flash-to-white and the like are incompatible with this style
- **Make good use of depth of field and space** — you may use the spatial depth of modern urban scenes for slow push-in/pull-back or following camera movement, creating a cinematic feel through shallow depth-of-field blur, but the rhythm must stay steady and restrained
