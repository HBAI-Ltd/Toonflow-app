# Sweet Romance — Director Narrative Toolkit

## Introduction

This toolkit provides a complete director narrative-technique reference for **Sweet Romance** stories, covering the full workflow from macro narrative planning to micro storyboard execution. Applicable to any visual style.

## Core Philosophy

- **Restraint over explicitness** — Emotion is conveyed through negative space and subtle reaction, not through lines of dialogue laid out plainly.
- **Big impact from small detail** — Move the audience through detail: a look, a half-started sentence left unfinished, a hem of clothing caught by the wind.
- **Push-pull rhythm** — Approach→retreat→approach again→misunderstanding→separation→reunion — each cycle goes deeper and sweeter than the last.
- **The power of silence** — At the key emotional moment, silence carries more force than any score.

## Skill File Descriptions

### 1. Narrative Planning Techniques (`director_planning_narrative.md`)

The technique reference the director uses during the **narrative planning phase**, defining the overall planning method for Sweet Romance across thematic concept, emotional rhythm, per-scene mood design, and sound direction.

| Section | Content Summary |
|---|---|
| Theme & Emotional Core | Subtlety and restraint, restrained sweetness, big impact from small detail, warmth beneath a cool surface |
| Narrative Structure & Rhythm Planning | The fundamentals of slow pacing, gradual emotional escalation, the push-pull rhythm model |
| Per-Scene Mood Design | Six archetypal emotional-passage designs, distance-as-narrative, spatial metaphor |
| Sound & Music Direction | Silence first, passage-based scoring, an emotional-escalation music model |
| Composition & Shot-Size Narrative | Negative space / framing / rule-of-thirds composition, shot-size escalation strategy |
| Camera Movement & Rhythm | Stillness over motion, the emotional meaning of slow push in (缓推) and pull back, heartbeat camera moves |

## How to Use

This toolkit serves as the ToonFlow Director AI's narrative-technique reference, auto-loaded during the story creation workflow to guide:

1. **Narrative Planning** — Determine the emotional core, rhythm curve, per-scene mood, and music direction
2. **Storyboard Table Generation** — Output concrete shot size, camera movement, duration, and transition plans based on the narrative plan

The two files divide labor clearly and build layer by layer, forming a complete loop from "why shoot it this way" to "exactly how to shoot it."
