# Comedy — Director Narrative Toolkit

## Introduction

This toolkit provides a complete director narrative-technique reference for **Comedy** stories, covering the full workflow from macro narrative planning to micro storyboard execution. Applicable to any visual style.

## Core Philosophy

- **Violated expectation** — The essence of a joke is "the audience expects X, but gets not-X." The bigger the gap, the bigger the laugh.
- **Rhythm is the funny** — The same joke lands as a scream or as an awkward silence depending only on timing. Timing is everything.
- **Character before gag** — The highest form of comedy isn't a clever one-liner; it's the absurdity that erupts naturally from characters' personalities colliding.
- **Laughter with tears** — Pure comedy fatigues fast. Bury real emotion inside the absurdity so the laughter suddenly turns into being moved.

## How to Use

This toolkit serves as the ToonFlow Director AI's narrative-technique reference, auto-loaded during the story creation workflow to guide:

1. **Narrative Planning** — Determine the comic core, joke-rhythm curve, per-scene comedy, and sound-effect timing
2. **Storyboard Table Generation** — Output concrete shot size, camera movement, duration, and transition plans based on the narrative plan

The two files divide labor clearly and build layer by layer, forming a complete loop from "why shoot it this way" to "exactly how to shoot it."
