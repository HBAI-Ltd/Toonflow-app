# Urban Workplace Drama — Director Narrative Toolkit

## Introduction

This toolkit provides a complete director narrative-technique reference for **Urban Workplace Drama** stories, covering the full workflow from macro narrative planning to micro storyboard execution. Applicable to any visual style.

## Core Philosophy

- **Authenticity is power** — The killer feature of workplace narrative is "realism" — letting the audience see themselves in the character.
- **Undercurrents of power play** — Polite on the surface, sparring underneath: workplace conflict isn't fought with fists, but over voice and leverage.
- **The cost of growth** — Every level-up has a price (compromise / loss / self-doubt); the growth arc isn't a straight climb, it's a spiral.
- **The light of the small person** — What moves us most isn't the top boss's scheming, but an ordinary person's quiet persistence in holding onto themselves within the system.

## How to Use

This toolkit serves as the ToonFlow Director AI's narrative-technique reference, auto-loaded during the story creation workflow to guide:

1. **Narrative Planning** — Determine the workplace core, power-play rhythm curve, scene power relationships, and sound-effect direction
2. **Storyboard Table Generation** — Output concrete shot size, camera movement, duration, and transition plans based on the narrative plan

The two files divide labor clearly and build layer by layer, forming a complete loop from "why shoot it this way" to "exactly how to shoot it."
