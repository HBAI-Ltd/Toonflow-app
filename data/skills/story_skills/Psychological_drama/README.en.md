# Psychological Drama — Director Narrative Toolkit

## Introduction

This toolkit provides a complete director narrative-technique reference for **Psychological Drama** stories, covering the full workflow from macro narrative planning to micro storyboard execution. Applicable to any visual style.

## Core Philosophy

- **Calm surface, churning undercurrent** — The core appeal of psychological drama is that "what's said and what's thought are never the same"; every line has a subtext, every smile might be a trap.
- **Information is a weapon** — Whoever holds the information holds the initiative; narrative tension comes from the asymmetry, concealment, and exposure of information.
- **Multi-layer game theory** — The highest form of psychological drama isn't A deceiving B, but the nested cognition of "A knows B is lying, B knows A knows, A knows B knows A knows."
- **The grey shades of human nature** — There is no absolute good or evil in psychological-drama narrative; every character has their own logic and their own reasons.
