# Historical Epic — Director Narrative Toolkit

## Introduction

This toolkit provides a complete director narrative-technique reference for **Historical Epic** stories, covering the full workflow from macro narrative planning to micro storyboard execution. Applicable to any visual style.

## Core Philosophy

- **Small people in a great era** — The power of the epic doesn't come from emperors and generals, but from ordinary people's struggle and choices within the surging current of history.
- **The irresistible force of fate** — The tension of historical narrative comes from "personal will colliding with the tide of the era" — the more one resists, the more tragic and grand it becomes.
- **Ritual as weight** — Enthronement, oath-taking, decisive battle, martyrdom — the ritual quality of these key moments gives events their historical weight.
- **A desolate undertone** — The emotional undertone of the epic is desolation, not sadness — the grandeur of "doing it though you know it cannot be done."
