# Xianxia Fantasy — Director Narrative Toolkit

## Introduction

This toolkit provides a complete director narrative-technique reference for **Xianxia Fantasy** stories, covering the full workflow from macro narrative planning to micro storyboard execution. Applicable to any visual style. *Xianxia* is the classic Chinese fantasy genre of immortal cultivators, mystical martial arts, and destiny bound to heaven and earth.

## Core Philosophy

- **Mood leads** — The soul of Xianxia narrative is "meaning beyond the frame"; a mountain, a river, a wisp of cloud all carry emotional expression.
- **Heaven, earth, and self as one** — A character's emotion merges with celestial and natural phenomena: sorrow raises the wind, rage brings thunder, enlightenment brings sudden heavenly light.
- **Destiny and defiance** — Narrative tension comes from the pull between "fate decreed" and "defying heaven" — the more unbreakable the destiny, the more moving the resistance.
- **Negative space is the Great Way** — The deepest truths, the strongest feelings, are never spoken — they live in a pot of wine, a blade, a silence in falling snow.

## How to Use

This toolkit serves as the ToonFlow Director AI's narrative-technique reference, auto-loaded during the story creation workflow to guide:

1. **Narrative Planning** — Determine the Xianxia core, destiny-rhythm curve, per-scene mood, and music direction
2. **Storyboard Table Generation** — Output concrete shot size, camera movement, duration, and transition plans based on the narrative plan

The two files divide labor clearly and build layer by layer, forming a complete loop from "why shoot it this way" to "exactly how to shoot it."
