# Storyboard-table narrative technique · Xianxia Fantasy · technique reference

---

## 1. What the storyboard table is for

The storyboard table is the director's core tool for turning the script into shot language. The fields of the form are set by the director as the project requires (shot number, shot size, camera movement, duration, characters, events, dialogue, lighting, emotion, transitions and so on); what follows is only a technique reference for the xianxia narrative genre.

---

## 2. Choosing the shot size

- **远景 is the base shot size of xianxia** — 远景 (long shot) is used far more often in xianxia narrative than in any other genre. A tiny human figure inside the vastness of heaven and earth is already carrying the xianxia philosophy; do not be stingy with 远景
- **中景 in the spirit** — 中景 (medium shot) in xianxia does not chase information density, it chases the mood of "a person inside a painting". The character merges with the environment, and the mountains, water, flowers and trees are all part of the picture
- **特写 of spirit and charm** — what 特写 (close-up) focuses on in xianxia is not pure expression but "spirit and charm": stars turning in the eyes, spiritual power flickering at the fingertips, light flowing along a blade. A 特写 must have a quality beyond the ordinary
- **大特写 held to the utmost** — 大特写 (extreme close-up) in xianxia is used at most once per passage, saved for the instant of "seeing the world in a grain": a single tear, a petal, a strand of severed hair
- **The establishing shot must be poetic** — a xianxia establishing shot does not only state the place, it must carry the mood: an immortal mountain in a sea of cloud, a lone pavilion under the moon, an ancient temple under snow. Complete the establishing and the mood in 1-2 远景 shots

---

## 3. Camera-movement rhythm

- **Mostly still** — 70% or more of the shots are 静止 (static) or move extremely slowly, letting the beauty and the mood of the picture itself speak
- **Riding-the-wind tracking** — use smooth tracking for passages of flight and leaping, holding the picture steady, building the composure of riding the wind rather than the scramble of a chase
- **Slow pan = unrolling the scroll** — a slow horizontal pan showing the whole of the landscape, extremely slow, like a painted scroll opening. It suits establishing and mood passages
- **缓推 = entering** — 缓推 (slow push in) from a 远景 to the character; the audience "walks into" the world of the picture. It suits the opening of a passage and enlightenment passages
- **Slow pull back = leaving the world** — pulling slowly from the character out to 远景, the character merging into heaven and earth. It suits parting, transcendence and ascension passages
- **升降 = elevation / falling** — the camera rising = ascension / realisation / transcendence; the camera descending = entering the world / falling / returning to the mortal. In xianxia the 升降 camera movement is a core narrative tool

---

## 4. Duration control

- **Take mood shots at ease** — give landscape empty shots and static cultivation shots a full 5-6s, so the audience can "savour" the mood; do not rush to cut away
- **Normal rhythm for character interaction** — narrative shots such as dialogue and interaction hold the normal 3-4s rhythm
- **One move, one shot in a fight** — a xianxia fight lives on the ceremony of each move and each form; every move gets a complete shot (3-5s) with no fragmented cutting
- **Enlightenment shots may run very long** — a shot of a character entering meditation or reaching realisation may exceed the 6s limit up to 8s, because "realisation" is itself a process that takes time. Pair it with a 缓推 or a 静止 camera
- **The golden 6-second rule applies flexibly** — in xianxia narrative a pure mood shot may be relaxed to 6-8s, but only on condition that the picture has natural movement (wind, cloud, water, changing light) to hold the visual interest
- **Controlling the time of "one passing thought"** — the instant of a key choice may be stretched with overcranking to 4-6s (the real action may last only 1s), magnifying the character's one passing thought into a thinking process the audience can perceive

---

## 5. Designing immortal-arts combat

- **Spirit before literalness** — the shots of a xianxia fight do not chase "seeing every punch clearly", they carry the "intent" of the move: a sword sweeps past, petals scatter; a palm strikes out, mountains and rivers shake. The shot description leans on mood rather than the detail of the action
- **Celestial signs answer the fight** — as the fight escalates the celestial signs change with it: a move struck → wind rises; moves traded → clouds churn; the final blow → heaven and earth change colour. Mark the changing celestial signs alongside in the storyboard description
- **One move settles it** — the final xianxia duel is best resolved in "one move": both sides gather force → they hold a look → they strike at once → the frame goes 静止 for an instant → the outcome is revealed. Spareness is power
- **Looks traded inside the fight** — thread 1-2 shots of a traded look (近景 intercut) between the exchanges of masters, the eyes carrying the shared understanding of "we both know the next move decides life and death"
- **Do not fragment a spell one shot can hold** — a complete casting (raising the hands → gathering force → releasing) should preferably be done in one long shot with camera movement, not split into fragments

---

## 6. Relationship narrative

- **Master and disciple, the passing down** — a master passing on his power, giving a sword or leaving a last instruction is the most classic inheritance shot in xianxia. Complete the emotional exchange with a 近景 intercut, and focus the handing over of the keepsake in 特写
- **The bond of Dao companions** — the expression of feeling in xianxia is extremely restrained: watching the moon shoulder to shoulder without a word, a fragrance left behind as they pass each other, a single "you came" a thousand years later. Let the storyboard carry the depth of feeling through distance and what is left bare
- **Sworn enemies facing each other** — sworn enemies stand off in symmetrical composition, the shot size escalating from 全景 (wide shot) to a 近景 intercut, ending on a 特写 of the traded look. Confrontation inside symmetry is the ceremony of the xianxia duel
- **The narrative of the solitary walker** — a xianxia protagonist usually has many passages of walking alone. Shoot the walking alone in 远景 with a composition on the back, never giving the face, letting the solitude carry itself through the spatial relations

---

## 7. Dialogue and what is left unsaid

- **Few words, deep meaning** — xianxia dialogue should be as spare as a poem, one line doing the work of ten. "Years from here, mountains high and waters far" has more force than "I'm leaving, take care of yourself and remember to eat"
- **A long silence is cultivation** — cultivation, enlightenment and solitary-walking passages may be entirely 无台词 (no dialogue), running 3-5 shots of pure silence. Silence is itself the narrative language of xianxia
- **Put poetic dialogue over an empty shot** — if the inner monologue or the narration is in poetic language, it sits better over a 远景 or a landscape empty shot than over a 近景 of the character
- **One line, one shot** — an important line takes a shot of its own and does not share it with other dialogue. Give that line enough space and enough silence around it
- **Last words extremely short** — a character's final line in xianxia should be extremely short (3-5 characters), paired with a 特写 and a fade in the sound. The shorter it is, the more weight it carries

---

## 8. Transition design

- **Mist transition** — the most classic xianxia transition: the picture is covered by cloud and mist → when it clears there is a new scene. Natural, in the spirit, and never throws the audience out
- **The turning of the four seasons** — express the passage of time with a fast change of season (flowers opening → leaves falling → snow covering → spring returning); it suits a large-span transition between cultivation passages
- **Reflection on water** — cut in on a reflection on water and enter the new scene or the memory as the ripples settle. It suits a transition into a past life or a memory passage
- **Hard cut within the same scene** — hold to hard cuts between shots inside the same scene, clean and decisive
- **Let an empty shot breathe between passages** — insert 1-2 landscape or celestial-sign empty shots (4-6s) between large passages as an emotional bridge
- **Dissolve for dreams and past lives** — enter a dream or a past-life memory with a slow dissolve; the instant the two images overlap implies the interweaving of two times
