# Narrative technique · Xianxia Fantasy · technique reference

---

## 1. Theme, premise and the xianxia core

### Key points for xianxia narrative

- **The pull between the Heavenly Way and the human heart** — the core contradiction of xianxia narrative is "the Heavenly Way is without feeling" set against "the human heart has feeling". Cultivation demands cutting off the seven emotions and six desires, yet what moves the audience is exactly the feeling the character cannot cut off — that contradiction is itself the best narrative tension
- **Destiny is not the ending, it is the starting point** — "fated" is not where the narrative ends, it is where it begins. What matters for the character arc is the choice to accept destiny or to defy it once it is known. The more unbreakable the mandate of heaven, the more staggering it is to go against it
- **Seeking the Dao alone** — the ground colour of a xianxia protagonist is solitude: climbing Kunlun alone, keeping watch alone for a thousand years, facing the heavenly tribulation alone. Solitude is not tragedy, it is the cultivation itself. The narrative must let the audience "understand" the weight of that solitude
- **Suggested directions for the exit feeling** — desolate grandeur / transcendence / unresolved longing / release through enlightenment. Avoid directions that do not match the xianxia temperament, such as "sweetness", "lightness" or "comedy"
- **The great love and the small feeling** — the scale of xianxia is "great love" (all living beings / the Heavenly Way / justice), but what moves people is the "small feeling" (one person's waiting, one sentence never said, one keepsake left behind). Set the premise on the great love, move people with the small feeling

## 2. Narrative structure and the rhythm of destiny

### Key points for xianxia narrative

- **A thread of destiny running through the whole** — xianxia narrative suits planting the seed of destiny at the very opening (a prophecy, a celestial sign, fragments of a past-life memory), so that from start to finish the audience sits inside the tension of "waiting for destiny to come due"
- **The three-lives karmic structure** — xianxia is naturally suited to the layered narrative of "past life — this life — next life". The regret of the past life plays out again in this one, and the choice of this life decides the next. The greater the span of time, the heavier the emotion
- **Escalating enlightenment** — the protagonist's growth is not "levelling up by killing monsters", it is "escalating enlightenment": from obsession to letting go, from having technique to having none, from seeking immortality to questioning the heart. Every realisation should come with a narrative turning point
- **Use celestial signs and seasons to breathe between passages** — join xianxia passages with the changes of heaven and earth as a natural bridge: spring going and autumn coming, the sun rising and the moon setting, clouds gathering and scattering. It carries more mood than an artificial transition
- **The arc of tribulation** — the rhythm engine of xianxia is the tribulation: the tribulation of the heart (a test of feeling) → the tribulation of the body (a test of the flesh) → the heavenly tribulation (the ultimate test). Every crossing costs more and yields a deeper realisation than the last
- **In a climax passage, "stillness" outranks "motion"** — the highest form of a final xianxia duel is not spells colliding in great sweeping strokes, it is the silent stand-off after all techniques return to one — one sword, one look, one thought, and the outcome is decided

## 3. Per-scene mood design

### Key points for xianxia narrative

- **Describe the mood poetically** — do not say "beautiful", say "moonlight on the cold pool, pine shadows slanting across it". A poetic description of the mood guides the scene art and the framing far better
- **Typical xianxia passages and their design** —

| Passage type | Emotional direction | Narrative technique | Music suggestion |
|---|---|---|---|
| Entering the world / entrance | Transcendent + striking | Appearing out of the mist, sword flight, easing in from 远景 | Ethereal xiao and dizi, much left bare |
| Cultivation / enlightenment | Serene + absorbed | Sitting alone on a summit, training under a waterfall, meditating under the stars | Guqin solo or pure natural sound |
| Tribulation of feeling / bonds | Endurance + deep feeling | A touch that never lands, a turn and an exit, a keepsake handed over | Xiao or xun solo, grieving but not wounded |
| Misunderstanding / separation | Desolate + resolute | Back to back, each stepping onto a different road | Low strings or a monologue of wind |
| Crossing tribulation / duel | Heroic + compassionate | The heavens transformed, one person alone against heaven and earth | From silence to thunderous drums |
| Ascension / nirvana | Release + transcendence | Turning to light and rising, all things reborn, dissolving with a smile | Sanskrit chant / celestial sound rising then receding |

- **Heaven answering man is the narrative core** — the character's emotion resonates on the same frequency as heaven and earth:
  - **Calm**: no wind on the water, the sunlight just right, flowers opening and falling of themselves
  - **Grief**: fine rain coming down, leaves scattering, the moon covered by cloud
  - **Anger**: a sudden gale, thunder and lightning, the earth shaking
  - **Realisation**: the clouds parting and the sun coming out, heaven's light pouring down, all things aglow
- **Space is the stage of cultivation** — the spatial levels of the scene map the character's stage of cultivation: foot of the mountain (entry) → halfway up (trial) → the summit (enlightenment) → the heaven beyond heaven (transcendence). The higher and more open the space, the more limpid the mood
- **Write shot intent as "the state attained", not "the picture"** — "the 远景 is there so the audience feels how small and how composed he is alone against heaven and earth" beats "shoot the person on the summit in 远景". Once the mood is clearly expressed, the storyboard is accurate by itself

## 4. Sound and music direction

### Key points for xianxia narrative

- **Celestial sound is the score** — the best "score" for xianxia is the sound of nature: wind through pines, a waterfall, a wind chime, birdsong, a distant bell. The richer the layers of natural sound, the stronger the immortal air
- **Leave the old music bare** — do not fill the guqin, xiao, dizi and xun in wall to wall; one phrase, then a stop, letting the blank between the notes carry the mood. The blanks in the score matter as much as the blanks in the picture
- **Avoid modern-sounding effects** — synthesisers, heavy sub-bass and other modern sounds are incompatible with the xianxia temperament. Even in a fight passage, build the rhythmic skeleton on traditional percussion: drums, gongs, bells
- **The ceremony of Sanskrit chant** — important passages such as an ascension, a crossing of tribulation or a great enlightenment may use Sanskrit chant or wordless vocal to create the ceremony of "passing beyond the mortal world". No more than 2-3 places in the whole film
- **Music-emotion model** —

| Emotional stage | Music strategy | Coverage |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday / cultivation | Guqin or xiao solo, very low volume | Low |
| Tribulation of feeling / bonds | Xiao or xun played grieving, restrained and held in | Medium-low |
| Building / gathering force | Drum beats from far to near, building | Medium |
| Crossing tribulation / duel | Thunderous drums or sudden total silence | Extreme |
| Ascension / transcendence | Sanskrit chant / celestial sound receding | Medium-high → fading |

- **"One sound settles the world"** — the most powerful sound in xianxia is often a single isolated sound: the ring of a sword, the strike of a bell, one peal of distant thunder. One clear single sound effect at the key instant is more staggering than a full score

## 5. Composition and shot-size narrative

### Key points for xianxia narrative

- **The narrative function of the three core compositions** —
  - **Landscape-in-the-spirit composition** — a tiny human figure set into a great expanse of bare heaven and earth, carrying the xianxia philosophy of "heaven and earth are vast, man is a mayfly"
  - **Mist-veiled composition** — cloud and mist half covering and half revealing the subject, creating the haze and mystery of an immortal realm
  - **Layered-depth composition** — foreground branches and leaves / the character in 中景 / mountain ranges in 远景, layer upon layer; the stronger the spatial depth, the deeper the mood
- **The sanctity of centred composition** — in xianxia, centred composition implies "the mandate of heaven / standing alone between heaven and earth"; save it for ceremonial scenes such as an ascension, a crossing of tribulation or an ultimate realisation
- **The "reaching heaven" feeling of vertical framing** — use vertical framing for vertical scenes such as waterfalls, cliffs and ascensions, stressing the force of heaven and earth run through
- **An empty shot is not a connector** — a xianxia landscape empty shot is itself narrative: falling petals = time passing; a bright full moon = longing; a great snowfall = a heart gone still. Give an empty shot a full 4-6s
- **Shot size is the state attained** —
  - **大远景 (extreme long shot) / 远景 (long shot)** — the order of heaven and earth, solitary cultivation, the weight of destiny
  - **全景 (wide shot)** — the whole of the scene, ceremonial occasions, the standing positions of a stand-off
  - **中景 (medium shot) / 近景 (medium close-up)** — character interaction, dialogue, changes of expression
  - **特写 (close-up)** — stars in the eyes, spiritual power at the fingertips, the grain of a keepsake; the focus of spirit and charm
- **The function of 大特写 in xianxia** — not to magnify emotion but to "see the world in a grain": moonlight reflected in a single tear, a petal landing in a palm, the opponent's face reflected in a blade. 大特写 (extreme close-up) 2-3 times in the whole film

## 6. Camera movement and rhythm

### Key points for xianxia narrative

- **Stillness is the doctrine** — 70% or more of the shots should be 静止 (static) or moving extremely slowly; the temperament of xianxia is "unmoving as a mountain". The stiller the picture, the deeper the mood
- **Riding-the-wind tracking** — use smooth tracking for passages of sword flight and cloud riding, holding the picture steady inside the sense of speed, so it feels like "riding the wind" rather than "being chased by it"
- **Slow pan = heaven and earth unrolling** — a slow horizontal pan showing the whole of the landscape, like a scroll being unrolled. Control the speed so that "the audience has time to savour every inch of the view"
- **缓推 = entering / drawing near** — 缓推 (slow push in) from a 远景 to the character, simulating the sense of "walking into this world". It suits an opening establishing shot and an enlightenment passage
- **Fast cutting is very rarely used in xianxia** — a xianxia fight does not pile up rhythm through fast cutting, it works on the "intent" of each move. Even spells colliding should be complete shots of great sweeping strokes rather than fragmented cutting
- **Camera movement is the Heavenly Way** — 静止 = stillness / realisation; 缓推 = entering the world / drawing near; slow pull back = leaving the world / transcendence; slow pan = heaven and earth unrolling; 升降 (crane) = elevation / falling
- **The xianxia privilege of 升降** — xianxia is one of the few genres that can use 升降 camera movement heavily: rising from the ground to the clouds (ascension), descending from the sky to the mortal world (entering the world). The direction of the 升降 is itself narrative
