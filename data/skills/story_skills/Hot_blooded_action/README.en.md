# Hot-Blooded Youth — Director Narrative Toolkit

## Introduction

This toolkit provides a complete director narrative-technique reference for **Hot-Blooded Youth** stories, covering the full workflow from macro narrative planning to micro storyboard execution. Applicable to any visual style.

## Core Philosophy

- **Escalating fire** — Hot-bloodedness isn't constant peak intensity; it climbs step by step out of a low point toward an eruption. The bigger the drop, the stronger the burn.
- **Belief made visible** — Abstract conviction and will must be externalized through concrete action, a gaze, a scar — so the audience "sees" the resolve.
- **The weak overcoming the strong** — The core appeal comes from "doing it though you know it's hopeless" — the more desperate the odds, the more electric the turnaround.
- **Bonds with companions** — Hot-bloodedness is never a solo fight; a companion's trust, sacrifice, and return are the strongest tear-jerking fuel.

| Section | Content Summary |
|---|---|
| Theme & Hot-Blooded Core | Externalized belief, the weak overcoming the strong, companion bonds, growth arc |
| Narrative Structure & Fire Rhythm | Low-point→climb→eruption cycle, three-stage escalating fire, emotional charge-up |
| Per-Scene Mood Design | Six archetypal hot-blooded-passage designs, escalating-conflict narrative, space turned battlefield |
| Sound & Music Direction | Escalating drumbeats, silent charge-up, timing the anthem's eruption, restraint before the shout |
| Composition & Shot-Size Narrative | Low-angle heroism / high-angle desperation / symmetrical standoff, power expressed through shot size |
| Camera Movement & Rhythm | Speed-line camera moves, impact slow motion, orbit (环绕) ramping, eruption snap-push |

### 2. Storyboard Table Narrative Techniques (`director_storyboard_table_narrative.md`)

| Section | Content Summary |
|---|---|

## How to Use

This toolkit serves as the ToonFlow Director AI's narrative-technique reference, auto-loaded during the story creation workflow to guide:

1. **Narrative Planning** — Determine the hot-blooded core, fire-rhythm curve, per-scene mood, and music-eruption timing
2. **Storyboard Table Generation** — Output concrete shot size, camera movement, duration, and transition plans based on the narrative plan

The two files divide labor clearly and build layer by layer, forming a complete loop from "why shoot it this way" to "exactly how to shoot it."
