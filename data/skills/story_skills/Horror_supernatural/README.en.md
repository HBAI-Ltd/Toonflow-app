# Horror & Supernatural — Director Narrative Toolkit

## Introduction

This toolkit provides a complete director narrative-technique reference for **Horror & Supernatural** stories, covering the full workflow from macro narrative planning to micro storyboard execution. Applicable to any visual style.

## Core Philosophy

- **The unknown is the fear** — True horror comes from "what you cannot see"; the audience's imagination is far more terrifying than any image you could show them.
- **A crack in the ordinary** — The most effective horror isn't the ghost itself, but something "not quite right" appearing in a familiar environment.
- **Slow corrosion** — Fear isn't a single jump scare; it spreads bit by bit from a subtle unease until there's nowhere left to escape.
- **People are scarier than ghosts** — The deepest horror comes from the dark side of human nature; the supernatural is only the mirror that reveals it.

| Per-Scene Mood Design | Six archetypal horror-passage designs, stripping away safety, spatial alienation |
