# Sci-Fi Post-Apocalypse — Director Narrative Toolkit

## Introduction

This toolkit provides a complete director narrative-technique reference for **Sci-Fi Post-Apocalypse** stories, covering the full workflow from macro narrative planning to micro storyboard execution. Applicable to any visual style.

## Core Philosophy

- **Ruin aesthetics** — The wreckage of civilization is itself the most powerful narrative; one collapsed building says "what happened here" more clearly than a thousand words.
- **Survival as theme** — The underlying drive of apocalyptic narrative is "stay alive"; every choice, every question of morality and humanity, is magnified under survival pressure.
- **Humanity's laboratory** — The apocalypse is a humanity stress-test under extreme conditions: who will betray, who will sacrifice, who stays kind in despair.
- **The weight of hope** — The darker the world, the more precious a single glimmer of light. One flower in the rubble carries more narrative force than a hundred acres of garden.

## How to Use

This toolkit serves as the ToonFlow Director AI's narrative-technique reference, auto-loaded during the story creation workflow to guide:

1. **Narrative Planning** — Determine the apocalyptic core, survival-rhythm curve, ruin-scene atmosphere, and sound-effect direction
2. **Storyboard Table Generation** — Output concrete shot size, camera movement, duration, and transition plans based on the narrative plan

The two files divide labor clearly and build layer by layer, forming a complete loop from "why shoot it this way" to "exactly how to shoot it."
