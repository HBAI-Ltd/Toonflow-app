# Mystery & Thriller — Director Narrative Toolkit

## Introduction

This toolkit provides a complete director narrative-technique reference for **Mystery & Thriller** stories, covering the full workflow from macro narrative planning to micro storyboard execution. Applicable to any visual style.

## Core Philosophy

- **Information control** — The essence of narrative is controlling "when the audience knows what"; suspense comes from the information gap.
- **Unreliable narration** — The camera itself can "lie": selective reveals, misleading shot sizing, deceptive transitions.
- **Peeling back layer by layer** — Every passage reveals one layer of truth while raising a new question, sustaining the audience's urge to solve it.
- **Detail is clue** — Every seemingly unrelated shot could be a plant; the highest reward is the audience's "aha" on rewatch.

| Section | Content Summary |

## How to Use

This toolkit serves as the ToonFlow Director AI's narrative-technique reference, auto-loaded during the story creation workflow to guide:

1. **Narrative Planning** — Determine the suspense core, information-release rhythm, scene atmosphere, and sound-effect direction
2. **Storyboard Table Generation** — Output concrete shot size, camera movement, duration, and transition plans based on the narrative plan

The two files divide labor clearly and build layer by layer, forming a complete loop from "why shoot it this way" to "exactly how to shoot it."
